## Original passages worth pursuing

### Manny Tis: New skin, same nerve

I crawled out of my old skin and hung beside it, waiting to harden. A fly landed on the empty face. I had to watch my previous body get away with having breakfast on it.

**Still needed:** Find a brown Mantis religiosa beside its actual exuvia. The fly on the old face is the distinctive central contact, not any molting portrait.

### Manny Tis: Rain has hands

One drop struck the leaf hard enough to move my entire morning. I caught the second drop with both forelegs. It went everywhere. I was still holding the shape of it when the third one hit.

**Still needed:** Find actual water impact or droplets and reaching forelegs on an appropriate mantis. The watering-can/drying portrait in160 was insufficient.

### Manny Tis: The ant took the long way

An ant climbed my leg carrying a crumb. I lifted the leg. It waited until I lowered it again. I have eaten things a hundred times its size and somehow I am the one holding the door.

**Still needed:** Needs a visible ant on a mantis leg or a comparably direct supported interaction. Do not use an empty leg portrait.

### Cygnus Shy: Possibly a stick

Something long and brown drifted toward me. I bit it. It tasted of stick. I bit it again in case it was pretending.

**Still needed:** A mute swan biting a floating stick would make the suspicion physical. Repeated checking is fiction; the object/contact should be visible.

### Cygnus Shy: Get off

Tipped up for pondweed. Came back to the surface with a duck standing on me. It had both feet settled. This had been a decision.

**Still needed:** Needs an actual duck standing on a swan. Keep the literal body while looking; a nearby swimming duck does not supply this event.

### Nib Willow: The fork with the lump

**Still needed:** Strong original preference for familiar discomfort. Nib168 has a sharp sleeping-on-branch candidate, but its rights and exact pairing review are unfinished. Preserve later lump continuity.

### Nib Willow: A change of menu

Found a fallen berry and carried it up to my branch. It rolled off while I was arranging my paws. Watched it land beside the bamboo. Some meals are very determined to be bamboo.

**Still needed:** Needs fruit and the red panda tree/branch context, ideally the dropped object. A bamboo-feeding portrait cannot supply the failed berry meal.

### Juniper Ears: Chew quietly

Went to find my noisy neighbor. His entrance was empty. Waited, listening for the chewing I had complained about. He came back with a beetle and I sat much too close while he ate it.

**Still needed:** A fennec eating an identifiable beetle with another nearby would support the unexpectedly missed neighbor. Verify species and scene, not generic two-fox proximity.

### Fern Longnose: The wet queue

We sheltered along a branch during the shower. The one above shook herself first. I shook next. A great deal of rain reached the last coati without ever falling out of the sky.

**Still needed:** Needs a white-nosed coati group along a wet branch or a clear shaking/rain interaction. This physical cascade is worth preserving while the image search continues.

### Fern Longnose: She came back

Got behind the group at the rotten trunk. A familiar nose appeared beside mine. She took the grub I had exposed and left. I followed her, relieved and furious in roughly equal amounts.

**Still needed:** Needs direct coati foraging/food contact that supports the relieved-and-furious encounter. Preserve white-nosed species and existing group relationships.

### Wren Boulder: The landing was fine

Jumped down to the next ledge. My hooves landed neatly. My chin met a branch I had not included in the landing. Stood there with leaves in my mouth and made the best of it.

**Still needed:** Needs klipspringer branch contact or feeding immediately beside a ledge. The unplanned leaf mouthful is the payoff; an ordinary rock portrait is weak evidence.
