## Admission status

140 individually authored entries across 14 new characters are integrated in `packages/shared/src/sample-character-arcs-ocean.ts`. Four RSS characters provide 40 multi-paragraph stories. The other 100 entries are social posts. Under the owner's mixed-media approval, six entries use reviewed scene photographs and 134 use explicit null bindings for intentional text-only presentation.

Six exact image bindings have visual scene-fit evidence. Commons API metadata retrieval returned `You are making too many requests to the API` on the first attempt in this work session. API requests stopped rather than escalating the limit. Existing catalog rows and ordinary Commons description pages were inspected as metadata leads, not treated as visual approvals. A hash computed from an inspected original image's bytes is identified separately below.

`packages/shared/src/sample-corpus-ocean-media.json` contains four new GeneratedAsset-compatible rows. Nell's `93eaea49a5ea54cfbe91c1004ac9a24a66e271b0` and Willa's `94af8624565c4a94c21bf13f61bf19720f4e6024` already exist in the main catalog and are deliberately excluded from this addition file. Together these account for all six reviewed bindings. Vera's creator and public-domain designation were read back from her Commons description page.

The author read the current editorial guide, including both rejected bee passages and the requirement to record feedback before continuing. Predator error can be funny; prey suffering is not the payoff. No philosopher material appears in this batch. Approved existing Frogbert and Alma copy was not touched.

The review below distinguishes fictional incidents from established natural history. These are imagined recurring individuals, not observations of the photographed animals. Coordinates are approximate fictional homes within candidate habitat regions, not photographer positions or claimed sightings. Exact bathymetry and all remaining species-specific premises still need final admission review.

## Primary natural-history evidence

- Nell: [NOAA's Gulf of Mexico dumbo identification](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/expedition-feature/okeanos-ex1903-logs-june28/) identifies Grimpoteuthis at genus level, not certain species. [NOAA's diet summary](https://sanctuaries.noaa.gov/news/oct23/odd-ocean-critters.html) supports small animal prey. The initial draft wrongly implied direct marine-snow consumption; it was corrected to an encounter with falling material and benthic prey.
- Percy: [MBARI sea-pig account](https://www.mbari.org/animal/sea-pig/) documents seafloor habitat, fresh-detritus feeding, tube feet and juvenile king-crab hitchhikers. The paper linked there, [Barry et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/maec.12396), is the underlying association evidence. Individual affection and passenger recognition are fiction, not established conclusions.
- Vera: [NOAA swimming-sea-cucumber account](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/ocean-fact/what-is-a-headless-chicken-monster/) supports active swimming between benthic feeding bouts. [Puerto Rico image record](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/multimedia/daily-image-media-20210501/) supports a deep Caribbean home. [The bioluminescence study](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-marine-biological-association-of-the-united-kingdom/article/abs/bioluminescence-in-the-benthopelagic-holothurian-enypniastes-eximia/2F72BF0FE0787ADD9636CEAEC2D4D3AF) supports mechanically triggered light. No developed eyesight is claimed.
- Pip: [NIH Hawaiian bobtail-squid research account](https://nigms.nih.gov/biobeat/2024/05/research-organism-superheroes-hawaiian-bobtail-squid) and [the symbiosis research review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8440403/) support bacterial light and counterillumination.
- Tavi: [Australian Museum mantis-shrimp account](https://australian.museum/learn/animals/crustaceans/mantis-shrimp/) supports the group distinction and Indo-Pacific occurrence. Specific peacock-mantis-shrimp feeding, molt and construction premises still need species-level admission review.
- Dot: [Australian Museum Bargibant's pygmy-seahorse account](https://australian.museum/learn/animals/fishes/pygmy-seahorse-hippocampus-bargibanti-whitley-1970/) supplies positive species identification and sea-fan association.
- Willa: [Aquarium of the Pacific leafy-seadragon account](https://www.aquariumofpacific.org/onlinelearningcenter/species/leafy_seadragon) was discovered, but full retrieval returned 403. Do not describe that retrieval as a completed biological review.
- Nellie: [Florida wildlife telemetry evidence](https://myfwc.com/research/manatee/research/radiotelemetry-tracking/learn/) documents travel between warm refuge and seagrass feeding sites. The fictional home uses the Crystal River coastal-spring region, not open ocean.
- Rhea: [NOAA hawksbill account](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/hawksbill-turtle) supports reef habitat and sponge feeding; avoid transferring eastern Pacific specializations automatically to the Caribbean character.
- Iris: [MarLIN's common-cuttlefish account](https://www.marlin.ac.uk/species/detail/1098) supports southern British habitat. Wrasse-following and particular social scenes remain fictional behavior requiring photographic support, not findings established by this account.
- Rue: [Australian Museum ribbon-eel account](https://australian.museum/learn/animals/fishes/ribbon-eel-rhinomuraena-quaesita/) confirms sandy/rubbly reef burrows, Indo-West Pacific range and the blue adult-male body. Do not mix black juveniles or yellow females into his unchanging adult arc.
- Rollo: [Museums Victoria's Fishes of Australia account](https://fishesofaustralia.net.au/home/species/850) confirms mollusc and other invertebrate feeding and northern Great Barrier Reef occurrence. His initial Mo'orea location was beyond this source's stated eastward range, so his home was moved to reef near Lizard Island. [Natural History Museum's inflation account](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/pufferfish-underwater-balloon-of-death.html) includes blackspotted puffer defense. Cleaner-mouth scenes still require direct evidence.
- Basil and Clove's additional species and habitat checks appear below. Individual fictional incidents are not represented as scientific observations.

## Editorial comparison pass

All 140 ocean entries received a read-through for incident, payoff, viewpoint and sensory plausibility. This is separate from the structural count check. Targeted comparison against land and air drafts found two mechanisms worth replacing here: Rhea's rejected sponge followed the land aardvark's repeated tasting of a disappointing meal; Rollo's repeated stone examination duplicated the land otter's shell investigation. Rhea now follows an inconveniently attached strip of food. Rollo now notices a bodily pleasure without trying to eat it. Other similarities remain at the broad topic level, such as awkward shelter, hunger and irritating companionship. The complete merged corpus still needs an independent editorial read; this pass is not a claim that every reader will find every entry funny.

The record avoids claiming that a source proves a fictional neighbor's response. Exact reactions and interior monologues are invented. Anatomical grounding was corrected where needed: no visual scene-reading for Vera, no marine-snow diet for Nell, no lobster claws for Basil, and no Moorea range for Rollo. The six assigned photographs have individual scene-fit descriptions above. Missing photographs will not be substituted with unrelated species portraits.

Second sensory review removed visual inspection of feeding tracks and visual recognition of hollows from Vera. Her episodes now use contact, tentacle sampling, current and body loading. The swimming sea cucumber must not acquire camera-like eyes merely because the narrator uses first person. Episode 10 was rewritten around suspension and an awkward mouth-first landing, rather than a generic observation of other animals' tracks.

Nell's seventh episode now has a failed grand arrival with a concrete feeding distraction. Her final entry reveals that the destination was the other octopus, through returning to a depleted patch rather than explaining friendship. Percy's ninth no longer invents a two-crab gathering unsupported by the reviewed photo. His final entry resolves the passenger's demands against his own feeding choice. These replace repetitive atmospheric bridge entries; final cross-corpus editorial approval remains outstanding.
