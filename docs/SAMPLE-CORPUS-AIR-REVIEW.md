### Final natural-history check

[Wildermuth, Borkenstein and Jödicke's common-darter study](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.2677710) confirms that heat-avoiding obelisk posture occurs in this exact species, but exceptionally. Aster's single hot-day episode is a possible individual incident, not a claim of typical behavior. The paper notes that seeking shade is probably the more usual response. Faye's roost use now has the species-specific source passage described above. Ellis's exact shelter details remain an open check and are not certified by a general family-level source.

Faye 10's first ending stated an abstract lesson about finishing. Self-review replaced it with a concrete choice: hearing the neighbor dismantle another nest, he closes his eyes and leaves his own imperfect work alone.
