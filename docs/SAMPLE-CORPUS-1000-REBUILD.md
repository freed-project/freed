2. Establish the character's personality, perception, relationships, and history.
3. Author a scene with a comic or emotional payoff that the image supports.
4. Review the image and text together. Reject either weak half.
5. Review chronology, biological plausibility, shared incidents, and repeated premises across the cast.
6. Admit accepted drafts into the runtime, preserving source and review provenance.
7. Verify exactly 1,000 entries, 1,000 honest image bindings, complete profile avatars, unique prose and images, per-character chronology, varied interleaving, and actual rendered media loading.
