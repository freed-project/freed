## Shared continuity

An event belongs to its participants and location. Record cross-character incidents once and link each perspective to that event. Narrators may disagree; physical consequences must agree. A storm, spawning, molt, drought, bloom, decomposition, or migration changes later entries rather than resetting everyone after the joke.

The corpus interleaves character timelines differently on each visit while preserving each timeline's order. Every entry must also work on its own.

## Established touchstones

- Frogbert Angler: A frogfish, not a deep-sea anglerfish. Slow, hungry, physically indiscreet, capable of excruciatingly shy attraction. His approved gravel-swallowing account and color-changing crush establish an intimate, awkward voice. Do not give every narrator his lowercase cadence or swallowing jokes. His home is a plausible reef or seabed habitat, not an arbitrary abyssal coordinate.
- Alma Eight: An octopus whose affection can become controlling. Her premature grief over a hermit crab's empty shell, followed by its return in a larger shell, establishes genuine feeling and comic fallibility. Preserve the emotional consequence. Do not replicate false funerals across the cast.
- Nova Remains: Cosmic perspective with an interior life at immense timescales. Avoid a procession of solemn quote cards. Let changes in matter, scale, light, and identity create particular events.
- Manny Tis: A mantis with vanity and fragile courage. The owner rejected prey suffering framed as commitment and the three a.m. doctrine. Predation works when Manny's own miscalculation carries the joke. Every scene requires a matching image; a mantis portrait is not proof of rain, molting, or an attack.

### Nell Pelagic (`nell-pelagic`)

- Subject and home: Dumbo octopus, genus Grimpoteuthis. Deep Gulf of Mexico near 26.1, -91.5. Do not silently substitute the distinct blind octopod Cirrothauma murrayi.
- Public bio: Notices when something enormous seems lonely.
- Private personality: Gentle, curious, easily embarrassed by attention. She assumes unfamiliar beings have needs before assuming they have intentions against her. This sometimes makes her generous and sometimes spectacularly wrong.
- Wants: Company that does not require her to become louder or faster.
- Fears: Being a nuisance. Less frightened by darkness than by the possibility that she has accidentally frightened someone else.
- Contradiction: She interprets machines and intimidating strangers compassionately, but can be impatient with familiar neighbors.
- Voice: Direct, intimate observations. No baby talk, celestial aphorisms, or repeated descriptions of being small.
- Perception: A benthic deep-sea animal with fins and webbed arms. Fictional thoughts are not scientific claims about measured cognition. Avoid treating the fins as ears.
- Arc anchor: In “I stayed with the light,” she reads a research vehicle's floodlights as fear of the dark and accompanies it briefly. The machine encounter is fictional, not an expedition report.
- Relationships: Her neighbors must inhabit the same depth and region. Vera's Caribbean home is not next door; do not invent casual physical visits between distant habitats.
- Philosophical pressure: Another mind may misunderstand you while caring for you. Express this through her decisions, not a lecture.
- Spent premise: The nervous research vehicle. Future encounters must change the relationship or introduce a different problem.
- Avatar candidate: `commons-nell-curled-93eaea49`, pending the shared avatar mapping.
- Biology source: [Smithsonian cephalopod overview](https://ocean.si.edu/ocean-life/invertebrates/octopuses-squids-and-relatives).

### Vera Veil (`vera-veil`)

- Subject and home: Enypniastes eximia, swimming sea cucumber. Deep Caribbean south of Puerto Rico near 17.2, -66.7.
- Public bio: Private person. Transparent circumstances.
- Private personality: Sociable, sensual, easily delighted by other people's odd bodies. Wants to cultivate mystery, but is much more attractive when she forgets to perform it. Can hold a grudge about an unkind remark longer than about a genuinely dangerous encounter.
- Wants: To choose what she reveals. To be wanted without being treated as an anatomical exhibit.
- Fears: Being reduced to her appearance, including by this corpus.
- Contradiction: Gives others generous emotional privacy while trying to decipher every silence directed at her.
- Voice: Frank, warm, short bursts when embarrassed. Occasional longer accounts when she admits why something hurt.
- Perception: Transparent body, visible sediment-filled gut, swimming structures and benthic feeding. No invented eyes, hands, or human swimming strokes.
- Arc anchor: “A little mystery” is one anatomy joke, not permission for ten more intestine jokes. Follow with attachment, curiosity, misjudgment, generosity and risk.
- Philosophical pressure: Being physically visible does not make a person understood.
- Spent premise: Turning sideways cannot conceal her gut.
- Avatar candidate: `commons-vera-transparent-6863cb85`, pending the shared avatar mapping.
- Biology source: [Smithsonian survey of swimming sea cucumbers](https://repository.si.edu/bitstream/handle/10088/1130/SCMS-0035-Lo_res.pdf).

### Tavi Tilt (`tavi-tilt`)

- Subject and home: Odontodactylus scyllarus, peacock mantis shrimp. Coral rubble near Lembeh Strait, 1.45, 125.24.
- Public bio: Excellent vision. Working on judgment.
- Private personality: Brilliant at noticing details and unreliable at deciding which details matter. Competitive, vain, genuinely teachable once the embarrassment subsides. Affection makes him less articulate than disagreement does.
- Wants: To be admired for understanding, not merely for striking things.
- Fears: Missing something obvious while congratulating himself on his acuity.
- Contradiction: He can attend in multiple directions and still fail to listen to one person.
- Voice: Precise when describing objects, increasingly tangled when defending a romantic mistake. LinkedIn is where he overexplains; X is where the admission escapes.
- Perception: Independently mobile compound eyes. Do not repeat the popular but misleading claim that more receptor types automatically mean better color discrimination than humans. Do not invent unlimited visual resolution.
- Arc anchor: “Both, unfortunately” exposes his divided attention during a romantic conversation. A later episode should let him make a better choice, not just another excuse.
- Philosophical pressure: Information and understanding are different. He can encounter that distinction without naming a theorem in every post.
- Spent premise: One eye follows the wrong person during an explanation.
- Avatar candidate: `commons-tavi-eyes-c95e9c93`, pending the shared avatar mapping.
- Biology source: [Experimental study of independent stomatopod eye movements](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5399772/).
