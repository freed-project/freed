## Mabel: A practice attempt

Proposed literal title: A practice attempt

I told him I could stay out of the water longer than he could.

He tucked his feet into his shell.

I tucked mine against my sides and slid off the log.

When I climbed back, he had put his feet out again.

I rested my chin on his shell and told him I hadn't started yet.
