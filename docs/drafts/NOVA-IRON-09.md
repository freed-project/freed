# Nova iron 09

Recommend the unannotated 3,600 × 3,600 Chandra 2017 Cassiopeia A element composite for Nova 5. Six candidate compositions were individually opened. No prose changes proposed.

## Protected episode

Title: Iron leaves home

> I scattered iron into space. Somewhere ahead, a world will put it in blood and call the pulse its own.

[Chandra’s matching 2017 account](https://chandra.harvard.edu/photo/2017/casa_life/) identifies the purple channel as iron, mapped through narrow X-ray energy ranges. Red represents silicon, yellow sulfur, green calcium; blue represents high-energy emission and the outer blast wave. This photograph therefore contains identified iron dispersed through the remnant, not merely an attractive nebula. The blood and world belong to the narrator’s explicit future. Do not claim that young Cas A supplied Earth’s existing blood.

The same source documents the massive-star fusion history, iron core, collapse and neutron-star formation. It supports the selected core-collapse identity. X-ray color is assigned to measured emission; these are not natural optical colors.
