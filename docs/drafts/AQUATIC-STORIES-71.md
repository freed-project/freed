## Literal proposed Story

**Hugo Rim: I had not meant to leave**

Instagram visual Story. Theme: `danger`.

My tail kept finding sand that would not hold. I pushed, rolled a little, and came back onto my shell.

Then something took hold of my rim.

Folded my legs tight. I had been trying to turn over, but I had not meant to leave the beach.

The sand came back under my feet. The grip was gone.

Put one foot down. Then all the others. I kept expecting another lift. Got all the way into the water on my own feet.

## Evidence and fiction

The primary observer reports finding the animal stranded upside down, turning it over, and seeing it walk toward water. Images show both orientations. The failed tail push, handling by the rim, defensive folding timing, fear of being carried away, incremental first steps and entering the water are fictional elaborations. Neither hands nor the lift is visible in the selected frame. The observer documents movement toward water, not final entry.

A failed effort to regain control is interrupted by an alarming lift. Being set down restores agency, but relief arrives one step at a time. This develops Hugo’s fear of restraint through an actual source-documented rescue, without another passenger complaint or mating grip.
