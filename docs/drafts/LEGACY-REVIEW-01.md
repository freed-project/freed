## Cross-entry continuity finding

The legacy Frogbert gallery mixes named painted frogfish (Antennarius pictus), hairy frogfish (Antennarius striatus), and warty frogfish (Antennarius maculatus). This is not simply a change of color or capture location. The current bio says only frogfish, but the editorial guide prohibits silently using different species as the same individual. The eight pairing dispositions below assess scene and prose; they do not silently waive that continuity rule. Parent should resolve the intended visual canon before counting these as fully admitted. Preserve the owner-approved hairy pair and painted-frogfish grey passage while making that decision. No mass replacement is proposed in this read-only review.
