## 01. Gentle with the little mouths

Replaces held candidate 01. Dot Thimble (`dot-thimble`), Instagram visual Story. This corrects the earlier draft's mistaken Dot Dapple lookup; it does not introduce a new character.

### Body

I found the perfect gap for a back scratch. Wedged myself in. Wriggled.

The coral folded its little mouths away from me.

I wriggled less. Then stopped. I am now holding the branch very gently, which is not how this treatment was supposed to go.

### Review

- Desire: Relieve an itch using a branch.
- Physical complication: Her rough contact disturbs the living coral she had treated as a scratching surface.
- Reversal: The animal seeking care ends up being careful with another animal. This is not another uncoiling-tail departure joke.
- Exact source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pygmy_Seahorse_-_Hippocampus_bargibanti.jpg
- Exact inspected file: `/tmp/freed-visual-ocean02.2gezcw/01.jpg`.
- Original SHA1: `6dd8b39047d8eee69e3116b9b2f5853e53bccceb`.
- Taxon: Hippocampus bargibanti, unchanged. No new color form or life stage.
- Actually visible: The seahorse presses closely against several coral branches. Its rounded knobs resemble the branching coral's conspicuous polyps; numerous polyps are visibly open. The branch-contact setting and the other animal's small structures matter to the story.
- Fiction, not photograph evidence: The itch, wriggling, earlier polyp withdrawal and Dot's subsequent gentleness. The image does not show active withdrawal. Do not describe it as a documented observed behavior. Lead may require biological confirmation of the proposed polyp response before admission.
- Quality risk: Gentle comic reversal rather than a hard punchline. Reject if it reads as a moral lesson rather than an interrupted bodily task. Do not add an explanatory ending about interconnectedness.
- Canon consequence if admitted: Dot becomes more attentive to the coral's individual polyps. This does not turn the coral into a named speaking character or establish a new relationship arc.
