# Bird regular 25: five candidates, eleven holds

Three conditional new identities: Agnes Splinter, Otis Gulp and Maud Overhead. All proposed entries are regular posts on Instagram or Facebook. Five candidate pairings and eleven holds follow. They require independent lead admission. Thirty-three exact NPS originals were opened individually before writing; no blocked request was retried. No runtime changes.

NPS Gallery rights flags were checked against credits and the actual images. Five green-heron sources were excluded for conflicting copyright notices, listed separately in the media JSON. A Public domain/Full flag alone did not clear them. No green-heron profile is proposed.

Some candidate sources support an imagined sequence rather than photographing every beat. Each record separates the visible central scene from fiction. Unrecorded sex, age and relationships remain unrecorded.
