## Held prose: With less of my weight

Unassigned southern Tanner crab. Facebook regular post, transformation. Preserved as a private draft only.

I pulled one claw free and put it down on the smooth floor to push.

It gave beneath me.

For a moment I stopped trying to get out. The shell I was leaving had never felt so dependable.

Moved the claw back. Worked another leg loose, then waited, then another. Each new part of me had to be kept out of the way while I finished becoming it.

When the last leg came free, I stayed beside the empty shell. Tried the floor again, with less of my weight.

It held.
