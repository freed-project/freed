# Botanical visual 27

Proposal only. Ten individually authored visual Story candidates: nine recommended for independent review and one held for image size. Two new identities, plus an explicit childhood scene for established Ambrose. Runtime remains untouched. Read [canon](BOTANICAL-VISUAL-27-CANON.md) before admission. Category is the supported `wildlife`; subjects remain botanical.

Twenty-two exact originals were opened, within the forty-source bound. Source inventory, original dimensions, byte sizes, SHA-1 values and exclusions are in [the JSON](BOTANICAL-VISUAL-27.json). Public-domain NPS gallery records require full granting rights. NPS page photographs have an explicit NPS credit. FWS source pages explicitly grant public-domain use. Four further gallery metadata leads were held without downloading: three lacked full rights confirmation and one was a video.

The real named sequoias and photographers retain attribution. Fiction does not assert that General Sherman and General Grant are one physical tree. Source images illustrate compatible fictional bodies and actions.
