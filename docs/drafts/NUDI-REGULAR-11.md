# Nudi regular 11

Eight replacement-slot proposals: five recommended, three held. This is not eight admissions. The existing approved mating episode remains slot 5 unchanged. The proposed juvenile, duplicate crowding scene and duplicate quiet meal remain outside admission until their stated blockers are resolved.

## Review basis

Followed the lead’s selected Nembrotha kubaryana identity and Anilao home. The bio uses borrowed chemical defenses. All 12 new candidate files were individually opened, with live Commons metadata fetched and original SHA recorded. Photographs from other compatible tropical localities illustrate the fictional Anilao life; they do not relocate Nudi. No unrelated genus, stinging-cell story or unidentified egg mass was substituted.

[Paul, Lindquist and Fenical 1990](https://www.int-res.com/articles/meps/59/m059p109.pdf) directly studied Nembrotha including N. kubaryana feeding on ascidians, dietary deterrents and defensive mucus. This supports the species canon, not a claim that every photographed tunicate colony has an identical chemistry. [The museum species account](https://www.seaslugforum.net/showall/nembkuba) includes feeding observations from the Philippines and describes the orange border and green markings. No proposed caption makes a fish appear in an empty frame.

The [2023 colour-ontogeny study](https://www.vliz.be/imisdocs/publications/392753.pdf) studied N. livingstonei and N. yonowae/cristata, not the candidate juvenile N. kubaryana. It therefore cannot settle the juvenile continuity question. The exact juvenile source says N. kubaryana, but this unusually different stage remains held rather than borrowing certainty from the wrong study.

Proposed event sequence: held juvenile beginning; fixed prey; held crowding; awkward turn; retained mating; held quiet meal; shared meal; underside search; awkward crossing. The held slots can remain absent. Five accepted proposals would give Nudi six reviewed episodes including the existing mating pair. Three movement scenes have different physical constraints, but the lead should still read their combined rhythm and reject redundancy if it dominates.
