## 01 Cora Curl: Back above ground

Facebook regular post. Adult female Furcifer pardalis.

I kept trying to hold the ground.

My front feet closed on earth. It broke apart. I pushed it behind me and took another grip. By the time the hole was deep enough, my tail was curled tightly around nothing.

Went backward into the dark. For a while there was only the wall against my sides and the slow work inside me.

When I came out, the eggs stayed below.

I pushed earth into the opening. More earth. The place grew flat and stopped looking like a place I had been.

At the first branch I reached, my tail went round before either front foot. For a while I stayed with all five grips closed.
