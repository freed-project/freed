- Body: the golden benthic dandelion siphonophore shown at Rose Atoll on February 18, 2017. Rhodaliidae family anchor, undescribed species possible. The family spelling is supported by [JAMSTEC BISMaL](https://www.godac.jamstec.go.jp/bismal/e/view/0000908); NOAA spells it Rhodalidae on this page. Do not replace with an arbitrary drifting siphonophore or a different dandelion form. The exact original upload thumbnail anchors the morphology.
- Home: deep rock slopes near Rose Atoll, American Samoa. Approximate fictional marker latitude -14.55, longitude -168.15.
- Bio: A dandelion siphonophore. Entirely sincere, in several places.
- Voice: affectionate and precise; uses I for the colony. Avoid meetings, votes, committees and bureaucracy as stock colonial-organism jokes.
- Wants and flaw: wants to promise total attention; mistakes an absolute declaration for something her distributed body can perform.
- Private belief: affection should survive an interruption. Her first declaration tests this rather than proving it.
- Relationships: an unnamed imagined nearby companion. No new named profile or mating observation is claimed.
- History and spent premise: first proposed episode, I mean every part of me. Reserve the feeding interruption to an absolute romantic statement; do not repeat it as a committee gag.
- Avatar: SHA1 2a8c53dba8e27cdf42fcdf7c071ff183743deb55, focal x .43, y .48, zoom 1.5. Full image and final 220/48 circles reviewed. Central float and clustered golden zooids are legible; thin outer anchoring threads naturally fall outside the circle.

- Voice: blunt about physical effort; affection emerges from an action he resists announcing.
- Wants and flaw: wants to be welcome, but normally behaves as though the seafloor and everyone on it will accommodate him.
- Private belief: gentleness counts even when nobody praises it. He is still learning to let that be enough.
- Relationships: unnamed local companion whose criticism is an imagined earlier conversation. Her presence is not claimed in footage.
- History and spent premise: I came softly turns a filmed landing into a deliberate act of care. Avoid repeated heavy-animal landing jokes.

- Voice: dry, patient and unexpectedly tender. Does not end every exchange by insulting the other animal's intelligence.

## Astra Bell (`astra-bell`)

- Body: the transparent cosmic jelly over Utu Seamount on February 21, 2017, with yellow internal structures and two spreading tentacle tiers. Rhopalonematidae source anchor. Do not promote the identification to Crossota alba: the NOAA report found during research discusses a separate south Manihiki Plateau observation, which does not establish this Utu animal's species. Never merge her with Atolla characters.
- Home: deep water near Utu Seamount in the northern American Samoa exploration region. Approximate fictional regional marker latitude -12, longitude -169; not a claimed surveyed coordinate.
- Bio: A small jellyfish. Beautiful until she needs to go somewhere.
- Voice: candid, interested in experience, intermittently susceptible to admiration.
- Wants and flaw: wants to move through the world but is tempted to preserve the arrangement that received praise.
- Private belief: she may change shape without explaining why she was beautiful before. Do not turn this into repeated inspirational maxims.
- Relationships: the delighted observers are fictionalized reactions to a visible machine encounter. No hearing or linguistic comprehension is asserted as biology; commentary is personification.
- History and spent premise: I wanted to move spends the spread-versus-contracted posture as a choice between admiration and movement. Future episodes need a new physical event.
- Avatar: SHA1 b7a3987f9e500dd2bc842e44d9e8b6c883fe3107, focal x .51, y .48, zoom 2.1. Final circle contains the full bell and most of the tentacle canopy at 220 and a clear luminous jelly shape at 48. The earlier crop cut the bell and was rejected.
