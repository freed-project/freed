## Independent second review

Opened the exact local `nircam.png` individually and read the proposed body beside it. Also read Nova's complete current seven-episode runtime arc and the central canon. This review does not verify the pending usage terms or admit the entry.

**Image-fit verdict: Hold this exact framing.** The pink remnant is clear and visually strong. The proposed light echo is the small gold, striated structure at the extreme lower right; the frame cuts through it on the right and bottom. Most of that feature is absent. At reduced Story size it reads as an unexplained corner fragment, while the unrelated central shell commands nearly all attention. A reader could easily identify the wrong glowing matter as the cloud described. The source annotation may identify the fragment scientifically, but that does not make this crop a strong illustration of the central event. I did not research or alter an alternative.

**Prose and canon verdict: Pass as a held draft.** The delayed arrival turns one catastrophic event into a later, awkward first encounter. “I would have liked to introduce myself more gently” gives Nova a particular desire without reducing the scene to a lesson. The physical action is light heating distant dust, not the shock physically reaching it. This differs from the existing unillustrated shock-heating passage and from admitted iron dispersal. “Then” is narrative order, not an assertion of instantaneous information returning to the remnant. Distributed awareness remains fictional personification. Keep the text intact while seeking a frame that can carry its cloud; do not pair it with a generic remnant portrait merely because the subject is Cassiopeia A.
