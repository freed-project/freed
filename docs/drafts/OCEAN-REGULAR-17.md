## Canon and source limits

Read the current complete Rhea, Nellie, Iris and Mora episode sequences, the relevant canon updates, recent Ocean12/13 and Mora15 decisions, and the editorial guide. Existing identities, voices and fictional homes remain unchanged. No protected passage is edited. These proposals add regular posts, not replacement drafts.

Mora remains Gymnothorax thyrsoideus at Anilao. The [Australian Museum species account](https://australian.museum/learn/animals/fishes/greyface-moray-gymnothorax-thyrsoideus/) identifies the pale snout, light-brown body and silvery eyes. Source01 hides the head but its photographer identifies the greyface moray explicitly, and sources02/03 show the diagnostic pale head and eye. The actual wrasse is identified as Oxycheilinus digramma. The source author writes Association?; this does not prove cooperative hunting, cleaning or any particular relationship. The conversations here are fiction, with visible proximity and orientation as their physical basis. Source photographs were made at Mabul, Malaysia, not Anilao. They illustrate the same species and do not imply fictional travel. No reproductive behavior or confirmed photographic sex is asserted.

Nellie remains the Florida subspecies Trichechus manatus latirostris at Crystal River. Source07 explicitly identifies that subspecies at Three Sisters Springs. [USFWS](https://www.fws.gov/apps/carp/species/florida-manatee-trichechus-manatus-latirostris) describes Florida manatee anatomy and behavior; the photographer's caption identifies the warm-water refuge. Source08 separately records manatee sleep in these shallows. The candidate invents an individual sleep-and-arrival sequence within that setting, not a source-observed chronology. There is no nursing or other newly assigned reproductive role.
