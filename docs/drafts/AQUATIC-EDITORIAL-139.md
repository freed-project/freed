Every time I came up, they were waiting. I took the fish deeper. Came up farther away. They found me before I had swallowed.

When it was gone, I stayed at the surface with my mouth closed.

One landed beside me. Then another.

I opened my mouth to show them.

They stayed. I stopped showing them.
