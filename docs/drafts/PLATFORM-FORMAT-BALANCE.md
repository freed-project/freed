# Platform format balance

Research checked September 5, 2026. The owner requests more Instagram Stories
than posts, provisionally 60% Stories and 40% posts. This is the editorial
target, not a claimed population statistic.

## Evidence and limits

[Metricool's publishing-frequency analysis](https://metricool.com/how-often-businesses-post-social-media/)
reports 8.85 Instagram Stories per week in its 2025 study. Its recommendation
of three to five feed posts per week is advice, not a matched observed
denominator. Dividing these figures would not establish an actual format ratio.

[Metricool's 2026 Instagram study announcement](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/)
describes 24,364,803 posts from 375,118 accounts in January and February of
2025 and 2026. It distinguishes Stories' role in existing relationships from
discovery formats, but does not give a comparable Stories-to-feed count.
Neither source establishes the behavior of all Instagram users.

[Socialinsider's Facebook benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/facebook)
cover 25 million posts from over 130,000 business pages. Its frequency table
separates statuses, albums, Reels, images, and links without a Stories count.
It cannot support a Facebook Stories percentage. Do not transfer Instagram's
target to Facebook and call that research.

Platform format history also constrains the mix. Official notices ended
[YouTube Stories on June 26, 2023](https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/217640760/youtube-stories-are-going-away-on-6-26-2023?hl=en-au),
[LinkedIn's Stories experience on September 30, 2021](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/linkedin-pages/organic-stories),
and [Twitter Fleets on August 3, 2021](https://blog.x.com/es_la/topics/product/2021/adios-fleets-twitter).
These notices support retaining regular formats on those platforms rather
than applying Instagram's Story target to them. A vertical video or Short
must not be relabeled as a Story.

## Authoring rules

| Platform | Apply to new content |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram | Prioritize distinct visual Stories until the accepted corpus reaches approximately 60% Stories. Thereafter aim for three Stories per two posts over batches, without weakening review. |
| Facebook | Continue both visual Stories and posts. Report the actual mix; no evidence-backed numeric target is established. |
| X and LinkedIn | Continue the existing regular-post format. Do not manufacture Story entries to satisfy another platform's target. |
| YouTube | Use actual playable video entries, first-person fictional titles and narration, then separated real source credits. A Short is not a Story. |
| Medium and Substack | Use articles. Narrative stories do not count as the application's visual Story format. |
| RSS | Preserve the corpus's existing regular-entry mapping. No platform Stories quota applies to a syndication format. |

Count only illustrated, accepted entries. Count a Story by `contentType:
"story"`, not prose length. Use each episode's platform override before the
character's default platform. A media catalog row is not an entry.
