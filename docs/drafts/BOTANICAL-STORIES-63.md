## 63-01: Sabine Warmfold

**Winter stopped there**

Before I had pollen to give, snow pressed against my hood. I warmed the flowers inside it, and the white edge began to withdraw.

For a moment I thought the cold was leaving.

The hollow widened just enough to show the mud around me. Beyond that, nothing thawed. I kept warming the flowers.
