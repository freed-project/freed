## The room behind the stone

I found the entrance with an arm. Sand, a lip of rock, then space. I put another arm in. Those two began exploring while I was still outside deciding whether to live there.

Getting my head through required a fold I had not tried before. For a moment one eye was outside and nearly everything else was inside. I had an excellent view of the thing I was stuck in.

When I finally squeezed through, an arm brought me a smooth shell from under the lip. Empty, I thought. I settled into the back with it. Two arms were still outside. I pulled them in and discovered they had brought a stone. Apparently we were moving in whether I liked it or not.
