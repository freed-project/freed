## 01. Berta Bank: The hard surface

Candidate. facebook post.

Came up where I had breathed yesterday and met ice. Pushed higher. It held.

I put both forepaws against it and swam along underneath. Kept trying to lift my head before I had found the end. Each time I did, I stopped moving.

Had to lower my face to go on. Then one paw reached into nothing. Turned toward it and came up breathing.

The opening was wider than I was. I swam around its edge before going under again.

## 03. Oswin Broadback: Four places to stand

Candidate. medium article.

I remember pushing up against her before I could stand without her. My front legs went straight. My back legs folded. When I got those straight, the front ones had spread apart.

She was warm all along one side of me. I leaned harder and stopped falling. Then she turned her head. I went with it, though my feet stayed where they were, and sat down abruptly.

Got up again. Put my shoulder against her leg this time. That held. I wanted to stay there, with all four feet settled, but she took a step and I was standing by myself.

I did not know which foot I could spare. Lifted one, set it down almost where it had been. She was already farther away. Lifted it again and put it ahead of me. Then the next.

Reached her and leaned so hard that she shifted her weight. Had to take another step.
