## 24-01 Hester Sweetlip: The useful edge

medium, regular article. Source `a2c2c97ca23c34848048b35258da3018dfb26a6d`. [NPS record](https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/407003ab-8bf4-4699-840a-616f5fa03339).

I had made the entrance sweet and the way down slippery. A wasp arrived. He put his head inside.

For a while I considered the matter settled.

Then one of his feet tightened on my rim. Another found the edge beside it. He lowered himself farther without giving up either hold. I had attracted an insect who understood the difference between visiting and committing.

There was no correction I could make. I could not turn the rim inward. I could not close the top. All the growing that had brought me to this shape was finished, at least for this particular pitcher. The wasp had found an advantage in the part I had been most pleased with.

He shifted his grip. I prepared to regard that as progress. He shifted it back.

Below him, the whole funnel waited. Above him, his abdomen remained in the sunlight. Between the two he had arranged his legs with an attention to detail I would have admired in almost anyone else.

I had supplied the invitation, the sweetness, the drop. I had also supplied a very good handrail.

Visual evidence: A red-brown wasp has its head lowered into a green pitcher while legs visibly brace on the rim. The final physical reversal is visible. Changes of grip, waiting and private certainty are fiction; escape or capture is not claimed.

## Source and review limits

Five scenes are distinct: braced wasp, butterfly on flower, ants inside the funnel, flowering nub and birds on arms. All are illustrated fictional individuals, not biographies of specific photographed specimens. Their inner speech and intentions are invented. Pitcher traps remain passive; cactus arms cannot gesture. The NPS source describes an unusual saguaro bloom, with drought stress only a proposed explanation.

The Medium piece develops the failure of a static trap around the visible grip. It is a regular article, not a visual Story or an actual publication. If the lead finds the waiting beats repetitive, hold the article rather than promoting its platform label as proof of substance.

No prey suffering is the payoff. No named philosophical attribution, cross-character meeting or scientific claim of plant consciousness. The seven source rows remain unaggregated pending independent admission.
