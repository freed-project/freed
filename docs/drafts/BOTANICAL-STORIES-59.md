## 59-01: Delphine Silkspill

**The next mouth**

One of the aphids had a daughter without leaving my stem. The daughter put her mouth down beside her mother's.

I kept sending sap toward the new leaves.

It was becoming difficult to tell whose family I was raising.

Instagram visual Story; theme `family`. Established common milkweed, *Asclepias syriaca*. Her existing Minnesota home and seasonal perennial embodiment remain unchanged. This is a recollection during leafy growth before seed maturation, not a closed pod restored after release. She wants to direct her generosity; the colony makes her uncertain about whose offspring her body supports. The body was written after inspecting the photographs and reading her complete admitted timeline and Botanical27 canon.
