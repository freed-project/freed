## Literal proposed Story

**Mabel Lowwater: I called first**

Instagram visual Story. Theme: `family`.

I came out shouting. Nobody else had come out, which seemed like something Mother should know.

Then the egg under my chin shouted back.

Lifted my head off it. Waited for them to finish before I tried again.

## Source and continuity decision

Earliest childhood recollection, before admitted juvenile Story I was going to hide. Present profile remains an adult female. Source hatchling sex is unknown; female casting is fictional and does not contradict visible sex-specific anatomy. This establishes neither Mabel’s own nest nor her offspring. The unseen sibling is unnamed.

The still documents hatchling, open mouth, head position and eggs. Calling, a reply from inside an egg, prior chin contact, the misunderstanding and turn-taking are authored fiction. NPS documents calls when eggs are ready to hatch, but neither this individual vocalizing nor an acoustic exchange was recorded here.

Mabel mistakes the unhatched clutch for absence, starts an alarm, receives an answer from a body she had treated as scenery, and gives it room to finish. Movement is from announcing the problem to attending to a sibling. This does not cast her as another animal’s protector.

Audio carries the reversal in a silent still. Lead should hold if this is judged too much offscreen reconstruction. No claimed hatching sequence or incoming mother is pictured.

Primary biology: [NPS American alligator profile](https://www.nps.gov/ever/learn/nature/alligator.htm), read September 5, 2026. It supports the taxon, habitat, yellow juvenile bands and pre-hatching calls. It does not establish the invented exchange in this photograph.

Keep the existing adult avatar and all approved prose unchanged. No avatar proof is needed because no profile change is proposed.
