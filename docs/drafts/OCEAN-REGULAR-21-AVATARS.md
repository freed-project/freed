# Ocean 21 proposed avatars

All four crops were rendered with the current `cropDecodedGalaxyAvatar` helper in a headless browser, displayed at 220 and 48 pixels, and visually opened. The lead independently opened this exact sheet and approved all four crops at both sizes. This approves the crops only; character and regular admission remain separate. Exact source metadata and focal objects are in [avatar JSON](OCEAN-REGULAR-21-AVATARS.json).
