## How she says hello

When she came back, I went towards her very slowly. I could see the pale marks across her back.

She put her nose against mine. I barely pressed.

She asked whether I had forgotten her.

I said of course not.

She pushed harder.

I had been remembering all the ways I might hurt her. I had forgotten how firmly she liked to say hello.

I pushed back. She moved a little.

Then she came at me again.
