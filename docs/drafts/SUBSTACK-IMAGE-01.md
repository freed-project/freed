## 01. The long way around

Character: Romy Helix (`romy-helix`). Subject: Iridogorgia coral. Theme: friendship, bodily difference.

### Body

He asked whether there was a shorter way to reach my upper branches. There is. He could swim.

But he likes climbing, and I like knowing where he is. A little pressure travels along me, disappears while he reaches, returns somewhere I wasn't expecting. When he swims I lose all of that. He becomes a rumor told by the water.

I did not explain this. I explained the branches. He followed my explanation until he had gone almost all the way around and was beside the place where he started, only higher.

He called down that I had given him terrible directions.

I said he was making excellent progress.

There was a long pause. Then another small grip. Another reach. He continued upward, muttering about the shape of me.

I have known him long enough to distinguish his irritated footsteps from his tired ones. These were irritated. He had plenty left.

## 02. The resemblance problem

Character: Oswald Cup (`oswald-cup`). Subject: Caulophacus glass sponge. Theme: identity, companionship.

### Body

The lobster returned with unsettling news. There were two other sponges beside me, and we looked alike.

I asked how alike.

He said I would understand if I could see us. We had already established that I couldn't, but he enjoys keeping that possibility alive.

For a while I disliked the others. I had worked my way into this particular shape without even knowing what it was, and apparently they had arrived at much the same conclusion. I asked which of us was best.

The lobster went quiet.

Then he climbed back into the fold where he sleeps. I could feel him rearranging himself, catching one leg, tugging it free. He said the middle one was larger. The other had a nicer curve.

I waited for him to finish.

He didn't. He was asleep.

I have decided against asking the question again. There is a great deal to be said for being the one with his leg stuck in it.

## 03. Apparently I am scarlet

Character: Dorian Sulk (`dorian-sulk`). Subject: Chaunacops sea toad. Theme: discovery, privacy.

### Body

Until the light arrived, I thought the most conspicuous thing about me was my opinion.

The water turned bright. Every little ridge on my skin stood out. A passing fish stopped, looked at me, and said something about red.

I have spent a considerable amount of my life being left alone on dark rocks. Now I learn there has been a whole other version of me, waiting for someone to bring the right kind of light. Apparently that version looks enthusiastic.

The fish said I was beautiful.

I told her I had done nothing differently.

She stayed. I disliked the inspection, but I had also noticed that she had stopped inspecting the rocks. We sat through a long, uncomfortable interval in which I could find no way to make her compliment less pleasant.

When the light moved away, she went with it.

I am back to being difficult to see. I had preferred this until quite recently.

## 04. Beside the dangerous end

Character: Severin Quiet (`severin-quiet`). Subject: Bathysaurus deep-sea lizardfish. Theme: trust, predation, restraint.

### Body

The little brittle star stopped beside my jaw.

There is a great deal of me available. A long back. A tail that gives everyone ample warning. He chose the part my acquaintances generally discuss from farther away.

I kept still. This is something I do well, although usually for reasons nobody finds reassuring.

He extended one arm, withdrew it, tried a different direction. I remembered my last attempt at an apology, when every explanation had required more teeth. So I offered no explanation. I let him be small beside me.

After a while I began to feel remarkably gentle. I wondered whether he could tell. I wondered whether he might mention it to somebody.

He moved on without looking back.

I stayed in place, enormously pleased with myself, until a shrimp passed safely beyond my reach. By then I had been such a good person for so long that I was furious with both of them.

## 05. The holes are occupied

Character: Oswald Cup (`oswald-cup`). Subject: Caulophacus glass sponge. Theme: self-knowledge, bodily dependence.

### Body

My guest described the little openings in my skin. There were more than he cared to count.

I asked him to count them anyway. I had become interested in how much of me was missing.

He started near the edge. He lost his place. He started again, grumbling, and after a while suggested that he could plug one with a small pebble so we would know where he'd begun.

That was when I understood the problem with the question.

Water was coming through the places I had called missing. I had been feeding through them throughout his explanation. The more carefully he described my apparent deficiencies, the more thoroughly I was having dinner.

I asked him to put the pebble down.

He did. Then he wanted to know whether he should continue counting.

I said yes. I still wanted the number. I wanted it very badly. But he could count from outside while I got on with being full of holes.

## 06. I was already eating

Character: Cato Many (`cato-many`). Subject: yellow stalked crinoid. Theme: embodiment, manners.

### Body

Someone asked me to stop eating while we spoke.

I tried to explain that the conversation had begun in the middle of my equipment. My arms were spread into the water. The small things arriving along them were already on their way to my mouth. I could not politely set the arrangement beside me and return to it later.

He said I could at least make an effort.

I bent an arm inward. This brought its latest collection closer to the middle. He watched me complete what looked, from his position, like a deliberate act of insolence.

I was mortified. I was also eating something rather good.

After he left, I kept thinking of the explanation I should have given. It became clearer and clearer. I would begin with the current, distinguish arriving food from chosen interruptions, explain the arm tips.

By the time I had it ready, I hoped he would never ask again. It was a much longer speech than lunch.

## 07. The reach of a compliment

Character: Cato Many (`cato-many`). Subject: yellow stalked crinoid. Theme: cooperation, vanity.

### Body

The little one below me asked whether my arms had always been this long.

I told him no. I was prepared to describe the patience involved. There had been a great deal of water. Some of it had contained almost nothing worth opening for. I had opened anyway, which seemed useful for a younger creature to hear.

He asked me to move the nearest arm a little.

I did. He asked for a little more.

I was pleased that he wanted to examine it properly. I extended into the current and tried to remember an especially lean period that would improve the account.

Then food began to reach him through the gap I'd made.

He thanked me and got busy.

I held the arm where he'd asked. There was nothing wrong with what he had done. It was clever, in fact. I would have admired the approach immediately if somebody else's enormous experience had been in the way.

## 08. Too many ways to answer

Character: Romy Helix (`romy-helix`). Subject: Iridogorgia coral. Theme: colonial identity, attachment.

### Body

The shrimp asked whether I liked having him here.

It was an unfortunate moment. Several mouths were feeding. One branch had his weight on it. Another was still irritated by where he'd put his claw earlier. A small part of me had been waiting for him since the water changed.

I wanted to give him one answer. That seemed to be what he was asking for. So I hesitated, trying to make the welcome large enough to include the annoyance without lying about either.

He began to leave.

I said yes so quickly that I startled myself. Then I added that he should lift the claw. He lifted the wrong one. I was irritated again. I was still glad.

He settled back among the branches.

I spent the next while wondering which part of that counted as the answer. He spent it picking something from his leg. Eventually he asked whether I could be fond of him a little more quietly.
