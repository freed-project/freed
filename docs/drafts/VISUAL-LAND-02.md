## 01. Which came first

Identity: Vesta Prickle (`vesta-prickle`). Platform: Instagram. Subject: spiny leaf insect, *Extatosoma tiaratum*. Theme: appetite and identity. Candidate.

Stopped chewing because the leaf looked a little too familiar. Then remembered which of us was copying whom.

Image: `strange-05-03`. Visible evidence: a brown spiny leaf insect hangs among broad leaves; its flattened leg lobes resemble the surrounding vegetation. It is beside a thorny stem. Chewing and the moment of recognition are fiction, not resolved in this photograph. The image supports the food/body resemblance central to the joke. Do not reuse the earlier thorn-snag plot.

Continuity: Vesta remains a herbivore. Her brief hesitation concerns resemblance, not harm to offspring, another animal, or her own leg. Her blunt correction differentiates this from a predator enjoying suffering. Scene-fit caveat: no feeding motion is documented; lead must judge the image as a paused feeding scene, not falsely certify that chewing is visible.

One loosened foot and its motive are fictional remembered action; the grip is visible. Red eyeshine comes from flash, not canonical eye color. This is not Rudi's reddish coat.

Continuity: Keep this short. Do not make it another dream about falling or an offer to rescue her. The affectionate joke is that bodily grip contradicts the attempted demonstration. No spoken exchange or predator needs to be invented outside the frame.

## 03. A wet attachment

The mud made a kissing noise when I pulled my hoof out. I took another step. It seems we are seeing each other now.

Image: `strange-07-09`. Visible evidence: horned adult male saiga stepping through shallow water over soft-looking sediment; one front hoof is lifted. The sound and tongue-in-cheek relationship are fiction. Do not claim a visible reflection, a trapped hoof, or courtship behavior.

Continuity: An adult bodily surprise, not another joke about Yuri's nose. This is the batch's one relationship/innuendo beat. If the last sentence feels too much like a human dating template, hold it; the beautiful image does not waive that test.

## 04. A quiet exit

Identity: Lucette Vein (`lucette-vein`). Platform: Facebook. Subject: *Chrysoperla carnea* group. Theme: physical scale. Hold.

I tried to leave without disturbing the leaves. One wing brushed an edge. An antenna found another. I have an impressively large amount of body for something you can see through.

Image: `strange-08-08`. Visible evidence: green lacewing resting on a leaf, exceptionally long antennae extending beyond its edges, transparent veined wings. The exit and contacts are fictional; no second organism, goodbye, or wet wall is pictured.

Continuity: Avoid the previously rejected antenna-goodbye mechanism. This proposal uses the mismatch between a seemingly insubstantial body and its physical reach. It is the weakest candidate: the last sentence may merely describe the photograph instead of rewarding the event. Do not count it unless revised or independently judged compelling.

## 05. Back and forth

A stem stroked my whole back as I passed underneath. I have now crossed this patch of moss six times.

The contact's sensation and repeated crossings are fiction, not a documented grooming behavior. Do not infer that the stem is deliberately scratching him.

Continuity: Gives Hugo pleasure in his soft body without a lecture about softness, a rejected new-house errand, or another complaint about being called a caterpillar. No romance, mating, or human contact is asserted.

## 06. A temporary loss of menace

Identity: Penny Focus (`penny-focus`). Platform: Facebook. Subject: adult female *Phidippus regius*. Theme: play. Candidate.

I was rehearsing a threatening gesture. Halfway through, I liked the stretch. We will resume being terrifying shortly.

Image: `strange-08-24`. Visible evidence: female jumping spider on a leaf with a front leg raised and extended, close view of eyes and fuzzy limbs. Rehearsal, menace, and pleasure are fictional readings of the posture. Do not claim that this is a documented female courtship display; the species-specific display described by the biological source concerns males.

Continuity: No forgotten speech, admirer complimenting her eyes, or unseen captured prey. There is no second spider in the photograph. Main risk: the threat-to-stretch transition is imagined rather than photographed. The image supplies the exact raised-limb posture that carries it.

- [University of Florida's regal jumping spider account](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/IN309) supports female appearance, visual hunting, and a distinction between male courtship display and fictional female gesture.
