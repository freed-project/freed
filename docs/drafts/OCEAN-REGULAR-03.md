Proposed replacement: `pip-pocket:1`, Instagram regular post. Childhood memory, not a claim that the adult has become a hatchling.

### Body

When we were newly hatched, I tried to get away from the others. Swam along the clear wall. Several followed. Turned. They turned too.

I thought I had become the one who knew where to go, which was worrying because I had just found the same wall again.

I spent the rest of my first morning trying to reach the back of a queue that kept forming behind me.
