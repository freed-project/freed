# Land regular 20: three proposed identities

Eight candidate pairs and eight holds. All are regular Instagram or Facebook entries, subject to independent lead admission. No runtime additions. Twenty-five exact NPS files were opened individually before prose; all six actual circular portraits were reviewed at 220 and 48 pixels. No Commons request or retry was made.

NPS Gallery asset constraints explicitly grant full public-domain rights for every selected source. Original files, source HTML, original dimensions and computed hashes are recorded in the media JSON. Files 20 and 21 are PNG bytes at NPS URLs ending in original.jpg; preserve the real MIME type. Album cover file 19 was excluded because its downloaded herd scene did not match its velvet-shedding album description.

A photographic individual is not the fictional character. Unrecorded sex or age remains unrecorded. These three species are proposed at species level, without subspecies claims. All before/after sequences and interior thoughts are original fiction.
