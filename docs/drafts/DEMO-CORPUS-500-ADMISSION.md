# Demo corpus 500 admission

## Decision

Admit 108 regular posts from `sample-character-arcs-five-hundred.ts`. Together with the 392 accepted baseline, the public demo now contains exactly 500 illustrated posts: 397 regular posts and 103 visual Stories.

The writing pass and admission pass were conducted separately. The admission pass opened all 108 exact delivery images, checked the existing per-asset creator and license records, read each finished post against its image and character, and assigned one manual feed classification. No Freed model, rules classifier, or generated runtime copy was used.

## Media review

The admitted set uses existing, attributable catalog records. No image binary enters Git. Seventy images form five-post visual sequences for nudibranchs, octopuses, sea turtles, cuttlefish, morays, frogfish, starfish, feather stars, mantises, moths, weevils, basalt columns, supernova remnants, and nudibranch courtship. Thirty-eight additional images extend distinct existing characters across birds, mammals, insects, plants, reptiles, amphibians, marine life, and one ancient tree.

The exact SHA, subject, finished prose, and classification for every admission are the literal records in `packages/shared/src/sample-character-arcs-five-hundred.ts`. Runtime admission requires each SHA to resolve once, requires an exact subject match, and rejects duplicate media.

Two initial sea turtle candidates were rejected before writing. One showed a turtle with a gharial in a captive bank scene, and one centered human photographers. They were replaced by the exact hawksbill feeding image `815f8035263d22aed8234c8e059a616566a51b7d` and green turtle breathing image `58113c4024c70176356feacb77ce09759f932a53`.

The remaining exact images pass. Their visible facts support the central moment in each post. Generic portraits are used only for immediate visible posture, scale, color, or attention. Captive provenance does not become fictional geography. Scientific and documentary images do not imply that a fictional character is the photographed individual.

## Writing review

All 108 bodies are first person. Titles and bodies are unique across all 896 authored episodes. New entries preserve the existing character, platform, home, relationships, and chronology. No new Story is admitted. No approved older body or media binding changes.

The admission rejects caption wrappers, species introductions, offscreen dramatic claims, lessons for humanity, and repeated punchline templates. Longer emotional claims remain grounded in the visible body or physical process. Predation posts place the comic pressure on the narrator rather than on prey suffering.
