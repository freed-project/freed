## 01. Berta Bank: Still attached

Facebook regular post. Candidate. Photo 34. Yearling memory.

> Found a knot in the fur under my chin. Worked at it with both paws until it came loose, then kept combing the place because it felt so good.
>
> There was another place just behind it. And another under my arm. By the time I looked up, the water had gone gold.
>
> I had meant to go somewhere. Couldn't remember where. Found the place under my arm again.

Visible: a yearling beaver bent into grooming beside golden water, forefeet at the chest. Fiction: knot, pleasure, duration and forgotten intention. The primary scene is absorbed grooming, not an invented unseen companion. Judge whether the sensory surrender earns its quiet ending.
