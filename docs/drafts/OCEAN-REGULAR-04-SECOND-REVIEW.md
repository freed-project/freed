Its beak nearly touches the surface, but the face is outside an open wall, and no narrow crack or shell-imposed limit is visible. The story's central physical obstruction is therefore weaker than its caption suggests. The opening also repeats the legacy feeding-crevice movement with curiosity replacing appetite. The closing explanation about having learned about herself states the insight after the bodily joke. Find a photograph that actually carries the cramped geometry before revising this premise.

### 05. The useful part of the branch: HOLD FOR A NARROW BIOLOGY REVISION

The original is a strong red-tubercled Dot-compatible close-up, with a visibly coiled tail and several conspicuous coral polyps. Its host dependency supports the ending. No particle capture or polyp closure is visible.

Shared food is plausible, but the specific feeding evidence is more interesting than a generic drifting-speck chase. Shepherd, Wandell and Ross observed settled Bargibant's seahorses taking trapped particles, Artemia and copepods from host polyps and between them, including inserting their snouts into polyps. They did not observe the settled fish taking suspended plankton in the water column. This aquarium observation does not establish impossibility in the wild; it does mean the proposal should start from food already on the host rather than imply an established free-water feeding mode. Keep the host-versus-lunch conflict, then submit the exact revised opening for review. Do not admit the current wording on the strength of a generic seahorse diet page. [Primary aquarium study, 2017](https://bioflux.com.ro/docs/2017.1049-1063.pdf).
