# Ocean regular 21: twelve proposals from sixteen reviewed NOAA sources

Twelve regular drafts across four proposed identities. Four additional photographs remain held. Nothing here admits a character or episode. Canon was fixed before prose in [OCEAN-REGULAR-21-CANON.md](OCEAN-REGULAR-21-CANON.md). All sixteen exact local images were opened individually. Their original published asset dimensions and byte hashes were measured locally. A filename containing 2160 does not establish a 2160-pixel original; source01 actually measures 1280 by 853.

These are illustrated fictional posts. Visible-scene notes distinguish the photograph from the imagined sequence, conversation and motive. All sources are NOAA agency assets with agency or staff credits. [NOAA media guidance](https://sanctuaries.noaa.gov/news/media/) supplies the government-image rights basis; third-party contest imagery was not used. Research permit credits are retained literally. No Commons request was made in this batch.

The target was sixteen fresh pairings. Four source holds and four conditional drafts are more honest than filling sixteen slots. The main unresolved issue is editorial strength, plus Fenn's Massachusetts source under an Alaska species-level fictional home. Keep that geography limitation visible. No subspecies or particular identified wild animal is asserted.

## Self-review

The drafts avoid shelter-fit complaints, shared-meal negotiation, cleaners gathering, phantom fish companions and generic declarations of species identity. Tobin filtering remains the weaker third whale draft because it follows another feeding event. Fenn's posture joke imagines discomfort without teaching an unsupported function for the banana posture. Selma's pup memory is explicitly earlier than adult life, and her female mother source is not replaced by an unsexed portrait. Della's calf interaction assigns neither parentage nor deliberate instruction. Source12 is visibly soft and must not pass solely because its story is appealing.
