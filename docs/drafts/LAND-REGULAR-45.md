## 01. Beatrice Bark: Still on the stem

Facebook regular. Independent adult-sized female American black bear, Ursus americanus, Great Smoky Mountains. Sex and precise age are fictional, not documented for the photographed bear.

I was going to make this cherry last.

The others had gone down almost before I tasted them. This one I touched with my tongue, still hanging from its stem. Turned my head farther around the trunk. Held it between my lips. Warm skin, then a little split, and the juice came out.

I wanted more of that. Pulled.

Swallowed it.

Kept my mouth open against the empty stem for a moment. Licked it. There was still a little sweetness there, so I licked it again, hugging the tree with all four feet.

Visible: the bear hugs a trunk and tilts her head sideways, tongue beneath a dark cherry. NPS explicitly describes eating a single cherry. Fiction: her attempt to prolong the taste, the split skin, sudden swallow and lingering at the empty stem. No claim that the photo documents a known individual or her life history.
