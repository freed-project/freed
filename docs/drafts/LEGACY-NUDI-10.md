# Legacy Nudi review 10

Nine exact image/prose pairs reviewed: zero unconditional keeps, nine holds. Slot 5 is a strong conditional keep only after a deliberate Nembrotha kubaryana canon decision. All prose remains draft material, unchanged. No runtime edits.

## Identity finding

Read Nudi’s complete nine-entry chronology, existing bio/home and avatar mapping, the character canon and editorial guide. The bio claims stolen stinging cells; slot 2 narrates hydroid feeding and stored nematocysts. The adult images span at least seven identified species, another tentatively identified frilled species, and an unidentified egg mass. They cannot depict one continuous biological life.

[The Australian Museum’s aeolid anatomy account](https://www.seaslugforum.net/showall/defcnid) explains storage of prey nematocysts in sacs at the tips of cerata. This mechanism must not be generalized to all nudibranchs. Sponge-derived defensive chemicals, ascidian-derived deterrents and stolen nematocysts are different defenses. The source photographs cannot be made biologically compatible by treating every bright slug as a costume change.

The specialist Sea Slug Forum sources below contain named experts’ observations and reported feeding records, with uncertainty preserved where stated. They are historical accounts, so source names are recorded alongside updated Commons categories rather than silently treating old taxonomy as current.

## Species decision options

1. Preserve the existing avatar and Anilao home by choosing Chromodoris joshi. The [museum account](https://www.seaslugforum.net/find/chrjosh) explicitly includes an Anilao observation and Philippine distribution. This is the smallest identity change, but the stolen-stinging-cell bio and hydroid episode must be replaced after review. None of the present episode pairs is admitted by choosing that identity. Do not invent a particular sponge prey for C. joshi without a species-specific feeding source.
2. Choose Nembrotha kubaryana for a rebuild around the strongest existing scene, slot 5. [Documented Philippine observations](https://www.seaslugforum.net/showall/nembkuba) support the region and show ascidian feeding. The account cites research on sequestered ascidian deterrents. A compatible proposed bio would be: “An Anilao nudibranch carrying borrowed chemical defenses, green stripes, and no patience for creatures impressed by shells.” This is a proposed canon change, not permission to alter the protected drafts or retain the other eight images. The current Chromodoris avatar would need replacement.
3. If stolen stinging cells are essential, keep the character held and source one identified aeolid with demonstrated prey/nematocyst biology and a supported Anilao range. Austraeolis ornata is not a shortcut: the reviewed species account explicitly leaves its feeding uncertain. No species is selected under this option.

Recommendation: the lead should choose between preserving the current C. joshi visual identity and rebuilding around the exceptionally clear Nembrotha mating image. Do not create seven new characters to rescue nine unrelated files. There is not enough reviewed story substance here to justify that expansion.
