## 1. Cleo Chorus: I mean every part of me

**Recommend independent review**

### Character narrative

I told him I loved him with every part of me.

One part caught something and began to eat.

He asked whether I meant it. I did. I would like him to understand how difficult an absolute statement is when this much of you can get hungry independently.

### Source and visual decision

The primary source identifies a possibly undescribed species in Rhodaliidae (spelled Rhodalidae on NOAA), attached by fine tentacles to iron-manganese rocks near Rose Atoll on February 18, 2017. The footage shows the golden central body, clustered zooids, contracting parts and long anchoring threads. Do not force a described genus/species. The thumbnail is the same visible colony and rock arrangement. Fictional declaration and partner are offscreen; the embodied joke depends on multiple feeding parts, not a photographed courtship. Final avatar retains the central float and golden zooids clearly at both sizes.

## 2. Boris Plated: I came softly

**Recommend independent review**

### Character narrative

She said I always arrived as though the bottom owed me somewhere to sit.

This time I swam. I lowered myself until the sand took my weight, then put down the legs one by one.

I wanted her to notice how gently I had come. For once I managed not to ask.

### Source and visual decision

The source explicitly identifies Bathynomus giganteus, nearly 30 centimeters long, near 810 meters during the November 30, 2017 South Reed dive southwest of Florida. Native footage shows swimming close above the sediment, settling onto it, then a close view of moving antennae and feet. The commentary uses an imagined prior criticism and a present gentle landing; no partner is claimed in the image. The thumbnail depicts the same armored animal on the same sediment. Final avatar gives a legible armored head and antenna bases, although its tiny eyes are not the distinguishing feature.

## 3. Giles Footfall: I can swim

**Recommend independent review**

### Character narrative

He kept offering to teach me to swim. I let him explain it while I walked beside him.

Eventually he spread his fins and demonstrated. Very graceful. He came back looking pleased.

I could have swum then. I had been able to swim throughout. But nobody had ever gone to so much trouble to spend another minute with me.

### Source and visual decision

The source explicitly identifies an armored searobin from the Ta'u unit, February 17, 2017. Genus and species are not established. Do not bind later generic searobin images without independent morphology/taxon review. Native footage shows separate stiff rays stepping over rock, with the pectoral fans opening beside the body. The thumbnail is an exact scene match. The fictional companion and lesson are offscreen; the contrast between walking ability and available swimming is the premise, grounded in NOAA's locomotion account. Final avatar retains the eye, snout, fan edge and walking rays at 220, with a recognizable fish head at 48.

## 4. Astra Bell: I wanted to move

**Recommend independent review**

### Character narrative

I held every tentacle out and drifted. Somewhere beyond me, someone was making delighted noises.

Then I needed to move. I pulled myself in and the beautiful arrangement disappeared.

For a moment I wanted to put it all back exactly as they had liked it. I kept going. There is quite a lot of water here, and I would like to reach some of it.

### Source and visual decision

The source identifies Rhopalonematidae at roughly 3,000 meters over Utu Seamount on February 21, 2017. Keep the source-image family anchor. A separately located Crossota alba record in a NOAA midwater report does not establish the species of this Utu animal. Do not map this animal onto Etta, Miri or Iona, all Atolla identities. Native samples show spread tentacles, contraction into a compact bell, renewed movement and a wider view over dark terrain. NOAA's 20201013 still shows the same recognizable two-tier configuration. The commentary is about choosing movement after being admired, not a claim that the organism hears human speech. Final avatar fits the complete bell and much of the tentacle canopy; its form stays clear at 48.
