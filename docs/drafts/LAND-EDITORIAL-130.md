## 01 Ada Dew: Before feet

RSS regular vignette. Earliest embryonic scene, before all adult episodes.

A small trembling came through the clear wall. Then another. I stopped turning.

The trembling grew until there was nowhere in the egg it did not reach. I pressed my face outward and waited while the whole soft place shook around me.

It went on for so long that I curled my tail back beside my face.

Eventually the shaking thinned and stopped.

Someone next to me wriggled. A little movement reached me through the jelly. I turned too.

For a while we disturbed only each other.
