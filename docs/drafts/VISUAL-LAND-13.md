# Visual land 13

Twelve new visual Story proposals for the four admitted land12 identities. Six pairings are approved by the lead for integration; six are held. All twenty-one source photographs inspected during this batch are outside the earlier land12 proposal set; no rejected pairing was recycled. Only Instagram and Facebook are proposed. Media metadata and source-specific age, sex and fiction boundaries accompany each retained image in VISUAL-LAND-13-MEDIA.json.

## 01. Berta Bank: The far end

Facebook Story. Hold for prose. Photo 1.

> Turned with the branch in my teeth. The far end swept through the reeds. Waited for them to stop shaking before I tried to look inconspicuous again.

An adult-sized North American beaver swims with a long branch projecting behind it. Exact action and taxon are supported. The turn is fiction. This still feels like an ordinary carrying mishap with embarrassment appended; keep held rather than using beaver identity to license endless errands.
