This revision removes the attempted capture of suspended plankton. Its feeding method also sought to leave excess food on the polyps for seahorses. That supports food overlap, without claiming the photographed coral stole anything or that competition was experimentally demonstrated. See the primary study below, pp. 1053 and 1054. The exact identity of the food stays unspecified.

The ending preserves the previously proposed bodily dependency joke. Lead gate: judge whether it lands as Dot's unresolved complaint or still feels too neatly constructed. This is not an introduction mistake, another fear of releasing her tail, or a claim that the polyp is intentionally obstructive.
