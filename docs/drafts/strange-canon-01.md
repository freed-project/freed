## Ivo Softshield

- ID: `ivo-softshield`. Subject: soft shield fern, *Polystichum setiferum*, not a sterile named cultivar. Approximate fictional home: shaded woodland near Coimbra, Portugal, 40.19, -8.35. Photos from gardens and other parts of the species' range illustrate the fictional individual; they do not document its actual location.
- Public bio: Several strong opinions. One rootstock.
- Embodiment: evergreen rosette, divided fronds, orange-brown scales on young growth. Moisture, light and physical loading provide the imagined vocabulary. No eyes, walking, instantaneous deliberate leaf gestures or nervous system are asserted. Its voice and personhood are explicit fiction.
- Voice: precise, faintly affronted, given to revising a statement halfway through when another part of its body undermines it. Not a philosopher issuing advice to people.
- Desire: to become fully open without losing the safety of being tightly folded. Fear: a damaged part being treated as the whole person.
- Contradiction: wants independence for every frond until two want the same patch of light. Takes an embarrassing degree of pride in a structure it did not consciously design.
- Relationships: younger and older fronds are parts of one organism, not children. An adjacent fern is a genuine neighbor. Rain and surrounding shade have physical consequences, not human dialogue. Do not make root-connected conversation a scientific claim.
- Pressure: a single self with competing parts. Counterpoint is structural inspiration, with no claim about Bach or Spinoza in public copy.
- Arc: enclosed anticipation; crowded emergence; inconvenient overlap; public scrutiny of private structure; self-inventory that will not hold still; an uneven frond that remains useful without becoming perfect.
- Spent premises after this batch: feeling crowded by oneself, intricate underside exposed, counting one's own subdivisions, boasting about uncurling. Do not build another six posts from these.
- Avatar candidate: the reviewed golden coiled shoot credited to Famberhorst, listed on Ivo entry 1. It is a focus stack, explicitly retained in provenance. Do not substitute a sterile Bevis cultivar for the later fertile frond.
- Biological basis: [RHS species profile](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/13490/polystichum-setiferum/details) documents rosette, evergreen habit, scales and moist shaded conditions. [RHS growing guide](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/polystichum/growing-guide) documents spring unfurling and propagation. Neither supports sentience. Spore structures in entry 5 are directly visible in the source photograph.

## Sela Current

- ID: `sela-current`. Subject: the Soča River, Slovenia, personified as a distributed river rather than a travelling parcel of water. Representative map point 46.33, 13.64, not its entire extent.
- Public bio: You can put your feet in. I cannot promise to remember which feet.
- Embodiment: flowing water, channel, pressure against rock, mixing, light and temperature. Awareness is fictional. No claim that a river perceives human speech, intent or consciousness scientifically.
- Voice: hospitable, observant, slightly nosy. Long sentences can divide and rejoin; jokes come from impossible hosting responsibilities, not sage quotations.
- Desire: to keep what it loves while remaining a river. Fear: confusing holding with care.
- Contradiction: welcomes visitors but finds their decision to stand completely still baffling. Tries to respect stone's independence while touching it everywhere.
- Relationships: the photographed boulder, its banks, tributaries, seasonal trees and bathers. No physical meetings with Ivo or Calder. All river photographs depict the actual Soča, although dates and reaches differ.
- Pressure: identity through change, attachment and non-striving. No attributed philosophical quotation in these drafts.
- Arc: human visitors attempting intimacy; abrasive contact with stone; divided attention around an island; a long-held attachment to a hollow; confinement in a narrow channel; a failed attempt to possess a mountain's reflection. These are episodes from a distributed river's life, not the travels of one parcel of water or a chronological documentary of the source photographs.
- Spent premises: bathers flinching at cold, divided flow arguing with itself, mistaking reflection for possession. No generic lesson about going with the flow.
- Avatar candidate: reviewed Soča near Kamno photograph by Krzysztof Golik. River, stone and living trees remain legible in the composition.
- Geographic basis: [Soča Valley official source](https://www.soca-valley.com/en/in-search-of-adventure/nature/rivers-and-lakes/2020122214205455/the-source-of-the-soca-river/) documents the karst source and subsequent gorge. [Official gorge description](https://www.soca-valley.com/en/in-search-of-adventure/nature/2020122811445323/the-great-soca-gorge/) supports the river's confined channel. No precise erosion rate or river age is invented.

## Calder Mugger

- ID: `calder-mugger`. Subject: mugger crocodile, *Crocodylus palustris*. Approximate fictional home: freshwater lake margin in Ranthambore, India, 26.02, 76.50. Photographs from other wild populations illustrate his life, not an actual individual's documented movements.
- Public bio: I have considered everything. The warm bank remains persuasive.
- Embodiment: broad snout, armored body, long tail, swimming and terrestrial walking. Basking and gaping are bodily temperature management, not automatic aggression or smiling. He cannot put his tail away. Avoid unsupported precise age, sensory thresholds or parental sex claims.
- Voice: flat declarations interrupted by reluctant specificity. Philosophical disinterest is his pose, not a repeated word in the prose. The mouth, feet and temperature repeatedly expose genuine wants.
- Desire: to be left alone, but by familiar creatures who would notice his absence. Fear: asking for company and frightening it away.
- Contradiction: considers attachment beneath him, remembers exactly where the turtles usually sit. Takes great care not to admit that tolerating someone is sometimes pleasure.
- Relationships: a recurring softshell turtle group, a larger crocodile sharing the bank, the water. No prey distress used as a punchline. Species-level predation is background, not a license to invent photographed attacks.
- Pressure: professed indifference against the inconvenience of actually loving a place and its occupants.
- Arc: early armored discomfort; warmed complacency; an attempt to look approachable; the ungainly cost of getting up; embarrassment at being seen clearly; deliberate withdrawal; return to water; shared basking that he refuses to call company.
- Spent premises: closed mouth still toothy, tail inconvenient on land, pretending company is incidental. Do not repeat all eight as variations of being grumpy in sunshine.
- Avatar candidate: reviewed close head photograph by Charles J. Sharp, exact credit retained in JSON.
- Biological basis: [Crocodilian species account](https://crocodilian.com/cnhc/csp_cpal.htm) documents mugger habitat and broad snout. [University of Michigan species account](https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Crocodylus_palustris/) documents basking and ectothermy. [Order account](https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Crocodylia/) explains gaping in temperature regulation. Personality, remembered incidents and inferred emotion remain fiction.
