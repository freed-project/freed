## 02. Maud Overhead: What I walked past

**Candidate**. Medium article.

I stepped over the dead frog on my way to the water.

There was a live one at the edge. I waited until he stopped moving. Hopped nearer. He vanished on the first hop. A little farther along, another gave me just enough time to spread my wings. I landed where he had been sitting. Water ran off a stone.

The dead frog was still there when I came back. Picked him up. Carried him toward the bank, where I could watch for another live one.

I had begun waiting again when I tried to open my bill. The frog was in it. I would have to put him down before I could catch anything.

Took him to a rock instead. Put a foot on him and ate. A frog jumped below me while I was eating. I watched where he went. Kept eating.

No mate, chicks or dependence on held aerial pursuit. The event tests her desire for command: she notices that pursuing a successful catch is keeping her from a meal already obtained.
