# Strange cast 03: draft canon

September 5, 2026. Image-led proposals only. No runtime admission, deployment or accepted-count claim. Every selected catalog photograph was opened individually before writing. Fictional speech translates imagined social experience, not a claim these species speak or reason like people.

## Vesta Prickle

*Extatosoma tiaratum*, spiny leaf insect. Fictional home: woodland near Brisbane, Australia, -27.44, 152.95. Public bio: I did ask nicely.

Vesta wants to remain a herbivore without being treated as harmless. She is theatrical under pressure and mortified afterward, then secretly pleased if the performance worked. Her fear is having to deliver on a threat. Her body supplies raised forelegs, leaf-like lobes, spines and a curled abdomen; it does not supply a venomous stinger. The story's imagined audience is not asserted to be in the photograph. Future episodes should explore feeding and reproduction rather than six more bluffing scenes. [Australian Museum insect guide](https://australian.museum/learn/animals/insects/) supports phasmid camouflage; the photograph directly verifies the depicted posture. Avatar and episode candidate: commons-0015.

## Nellie Notch

*Polydrusus formosus*, green immigrant leaf weevil. Fictional home: woodland edge near London, Ontario, 42.98, -81.25, matching source locality. Public bio: Still hungry. Different reasons.

Nellie is impatient, sociable and quick to blame food for being finite. She wants a satisfying meal and a witness who does not interrupt it. Her fear is being alone when something wonderful happens. She mistakes an appetite for an argument, but is more generous with company than food. Keep the short broad rostrum and green scales consistent. Two images show substrate and a leaf edge. Do not make this species the similarly green *Phyllobius* mating pair. Candidates: commons-0059 and commons-0101. Avatar: commons-0101.

## Lucien Reach

*Trachelophorus giraffa*, Madagascan giraffe weevil. Fictional home: Ranomafana, Madagascar, -21.25, 47.45. Public bio: I heard that.

Lucien enjoys being noticed until somebody asks a direct question. He is vain about the spectacular neck, uncertain what to do with the conversational attention it creates, and quick enough to recover with a poor excuse. He wants intimacy without surrendering his advantages. His fear is becoming a novelty instead of a person. The long black articulated neck, red wing cases and leaf are directly visible; do not assert the New Zealand giraffe weevil's different anatomy. Candidate avatar: commons-0138. Further episodes require fresh photos and biology verification, not more neck jokes.

## Ottilie Hook

*Curculio glandium*, acorn weevil. Fictional home: English oak woodland, 51.77, -1.32. Public bio: Closer is a relative term.

Ottilie is frank, practical and weary of men making the same observation about her face. She wants pleasure without having to explain her anatomy first. Her long rostrum ends in mouthparts; it is not a tongue, drinking straw, elephant trunk or hand. [The Wildlife Trusts species profile](https://www.wildlifetrusts.org/wildlife-explorer/invertebrates/beetles/acorn-weevil) describes the rostrum and acorn association. Candidate avatar: commons-0210. Sex is not asserted from the photograph alone.

## Mabel Mouthful

Prairie dog, genus *Cynomys*. Source title for commons-0937 does not establish exact species, so species-level admission remains conditional. Fictional Great Plains home is provisional pending source taxonomy and location. Public bio: Come here. No, before you go.

Mabel has two pups, Kit and Pip. She wants them to become capable without ceasing to need her. She disguises fear as nagging and affection as a last practical check. Mouth contact and multiple family members are visible in the selected photograph. [NPS prairie-dog account](https://www.nps.gov/thro/learn/nature/prairie-dogs.htm) documents identifying kisses among black-tailed prairie dogs, not proof that this unspecified photograph depicts that species. A family story can remain a draft while this gap is unresolved. Do not manufacture a species assertion to finish a batch.
