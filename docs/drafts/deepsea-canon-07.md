## Gilda Grip

- Taxon: Graneledone boreopacifica, supported by the source descriptions and NOAA Benthic Animal Guide.
- Proposed fictional home: Davidson Seamount, northeastern Pacific, approximately 35.72 N, 122.72 W. This is a habitat anchor, not a claim that every source photograph was taken there.
- Voice: full sentences, eager physical investigation, embarrassment admitted a little later than necessary.
- Want: to understand things and have someone worth telling.
- Flaw: increases the intensity of an action when it is not working. Eventually notices what this feels like to the recipient.
- Relationships: a coral colony she initially mistakes for one listener; a passing neighbor she wants to engage. Do not invent a father, childhood, or lifelong friendship to rescue an ending.
- History: tries to hold a laser spot, then admits to the coral that she went looking for it again. The coral's lack of judgment earns a confidence. Other entries explore effort overshooting its purpose, not another laser joke.
- Source locations vary between California and Alaska. These are illustrations of one fictional species-based character, not a real animal's tracked migration. No episode should assert interocean travel.
- Spent mechanisms: unattainable laser, collective confidant, forgotten-message pebble, overcommitted wave, overbearing apology. Do not create variants of these to fill the next batch.
