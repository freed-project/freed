## Biology review question

Discovery from [Steyn2018](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/ar.23733) supports scratch digging into hard soil and termite mounds. The [African Pangolin Working Group account](https://africanpangolin.org/about-us/the-four-african-pangolin-species/temmincks-pangolin/) describes small mound openings as rare and usually associated with rain-softened coverings. Independent review must decide whether the original phrase tear apart a mound overstates that behavior. No whole-mound demolition or routine frequency should be inferred.
