# Regular land 12 avatar review

All four proposed portraits were cropped from already inspected source photographs, rendered as actual circular PNGs at 220 and 48 pixels, and independently opened at both sizes. No generated pixels or new source photographs were introduced. Berta and Rufus use photographs whose paired posts remain held; portrait approval does not approve those posts.

Exact source licensing, SHA-1, focal coordinates, zoom, pixel crop bounds and output SHA-256 values are in REGULAR-LAND-12-AVATARS.json. Focal values match the existing cropDecodedGalaxyAvatar formula: square side equals the smaller source dimension divided by zoom, centered on the normalized focal point. Review images were resized and circularly clipped outside Git. The runtime must retain attribution and identify the crop as a modification where the license requires it.

| Proposed identity | Subject and stage | 220-pixel decision | 48-pixel decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Berta Bank | Castor canadensis, adult-sized; exact age/sex unverified | Face, incisors, whiskers and forepaws remain clear. Recommend. | Broad muzzle, orange incisors and fur silhouette remain legible. Recommend. |
| Euphemia Dust | Loxodonta africana, adult; sex unspecified in source and compatible with female character | Ear, eye, tusks, trunk and dust retain the animal's distinctive face. Recommend. | Ear-and-curved-trunk outline is clear; tiny eye detail appropriately disappears. Recommend. |
| Crispin Crag | Capra ibex, adult male | Tightened crop retains face, ear, horn bases and ridges against rock. Recommend. | Sideways head and paired ridged horns remain identifiable. Recommend; the pose is less frontal than the others. |
| Rufus Russet | Vulpes vulpes, adult-sized winter coat; exact age/sex unverified | Sleeping face, black ears and curled red coat read clearly. Recommend for portrait only. | Pale muzzle and black nose remain centered within red fur. Recommend for portrait only. |

Crispin's first crop included too much torso and left the face near the circular edge. It was replaced and both new sizes were opened. Only the corrected crop appears in the JSON. Rufus's source was photographed at Miyagi Zao Fox Village, Japan; it illustrates the red-fox body and is not evidence for a wild Ontario history.
