Eight bounded Story proposals for existing identities. One candidate, seven holds. Seventeen exact photographs were opened before writing. No runtime bindings or admissions are made here. The label includes the assigned marine and flying characters.

## 01. Nellie Nudge: The little ones

Facebook Story. Candidate for independent lead review. Photo 15.

> I lifted my flipper for one fish. The rest came through too. I held it there. There were some very small ones at the back.

The photograph shows a manatee facing the camera, one flipper curved beneath its chin, with a school of fish beneath and beside its body. The sequence and courtesy are fiction. The fish provide the scene's action and the size difference supplies its tenderness. The photo does not prove the fish are passing through in this order. Keep the entire school in the crop.

This is a new standalone interaction with fish. It neither repeats the companion-following ending nor changes the existing warm-water chronology. The source credits Ramos Keith, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, and explicitly supplies a worldwide public-domain author release. Preserve that displayed attribution. Current FWS pages independently credit photographer Keith Ramos and mark his manatee photographs public domain, but those separate photographs are not asserted to be this exact file.

## 02. Alba Longwing: Banking

## 03. Sorrel Spring: The folded part

Instagram Story. Hold for identification and prose. Photo 3.

> Crouched behind a pebble until the shadow passed. My feet were out in front. My tail was out behind. I had hidden the least of me.

A live jerboa crouches on gravel at night with folded long hind legs and a trailing tail. The source calls it a lesser Egyptian gerbil, and the exact Jaculus species needs a better authority before binding it to Sorrel. The shadow and concealment also remain offscreen. Do not substitute a different desert jerboa. Photo 1 is a specimen-like captive display and photo 2 is already in the media corpus.

## 05. Basil Backward: Across the gap

Facebook Story. Hold for image and prose. Photo 10.

The lobster is braced on uneven aquarium rock. No gap-crossing sequence is proven, the anatomy of the imagined failure is vague, and this image is already used. ## 06. Ludo Leap: A leg in the way

Instagram Story. Hold for prose. Photo 13.

> Raised a front leg to get a better look beyond the leaf. The leg filled most of the view. Put it down. Whatever I had been looking at had used the time well.

## 08. Alba Longwing: The new feathers

Instagram Story. Hold for action and identity framing. Photo 9.

> The chick shook itself and a white tuft came loose. I watched it blow away. Underneath was a patch of proper dark wing. I had been watching the mouth and missed this part.

The chick portrait is exceptionally clear, with white down and exposed dark wing feathers. The lost tuft and parent's observation are invented. A chick portrait alone does not carry the shake, and Alba is the adult narrator rather than the pictured individual. Do not change the account's age or silently treat this chick as Alba. A future stronger parent-and-chick image might support a different episode, but this one remains held.
