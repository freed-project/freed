## 01. Silas Return: What I remembered

Facebook Story. Theme: movement. Author image pass and prose pass. Lead independently opened originals11/12 and actual 220/48 crop, and approved the literal Story and avatar. Integration remains the lead editor responsibility.

I remembered enough water here to turn in.

My belly touched stone. I beat my tail and moved half my length. The water closed over my gills, then fell away from my head.

Another red back broke the surface beside mine. He pushed forward. I pushed with him.

I had come so far to feel this water again. Now I wanted a little more of it.

Exact scene: Red sockeye crowd a shallow gravel channel, with green heads, red backs and fins breaking the surface and small splashes among them.

The image anchors shallow water and exposed spawning bodies. The remembered juvenile turning room, belly touching stone, short advance and neighboring male are fiction. This does not claim a photographed rescue, cooperative pushing, an exhausted fish recovering, or documented intentions. He pushes himself, alongside another fish. The emotional turn is the return he wanted becoming an immediate wish for more water. It is not a second migration, shelter problem, feeding complaint or species-summary caption.
