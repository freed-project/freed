Two distinct Instagram Stories proposed for independent review after eighteen exact image reviews. No admission claimed.

## fenn-lowtide: The same bit

When I was young, a yellow thing came up under my chin. I pushed it down. It came back.

I put more of myself on it and went under with it. When it lifted me again, I opened my mouth to complain.

Then I pushed it down once more, trying to get the same bit under my chin.

Juvenile harbor seal lies across a yellow floating barrier, depressing it, with head raised and mouth open. Other exact frames show foreflippers resting on barrier and body pressing across it. No entanglement or injury visibly established.

Fiction boundary: Repeated submergence, rebound, annoyance and voluntary repetition are fiction. Source observer describes a playful child; three stills do not document full motion sequence or vocalization. Barrier buoyancy and flex are physical inference from floating/depressed material. Do not identify pollution, oil exposure, rescue or training.

Chronology: Insert before Fenn current sole adult episode The other view. Explicit childhood memory preserves mature ice-haulout scene and existing avatar. Source is San Francisco; narrator home remains Kenai Fjords. Treat photograph as species-compatible illustration of fictional harbor setting, not evidence Fenn migrated from California to Alaska. Source subspecies Phoca vitulina richardii is eastern Pacific harbor seal.

Independent review question: Seal is smaller within frame than a portrait, but body/contact and yellow object are clear. Root should judge exact scene at reading size. End changes pushing from rejection to intentional play; no rest-site logistics or protector beat.

## ada-spine: I still meant it

He touched my back and pulled away. I had forgotten the little pincers around my spines.

I said I hadn't meant it.

He asked whether I still wanted him close.

I said yes.

He is waiting for the rest of me to agree.

Macro of Marthasterias glacialis dorsal surface, with pale pointed spines surrounded by yellow cushions of tiny pedicellariae. Individual small oval structures are visible; entire star and visitor are outside frame.

Fiction boundary: Visitor, invitation, touch, withdrawal, dialogue and apology are fiction. The pincers and their local defensive function are anatomical evidence. No photographed bite, injury, voluntary decision by each pincer or experimentally measured response in this individual is claimed.

Chronology: Append after Ada current three episodes A type, Worse after washing and A small hello. Current adult female narrator and Arrabida home retained. Source Sesimbra compatible with exact home region. May read companion as unnamed star from A type without declaring confirmed mating or new permanent pair.

Independent review question: Image earns body mechanism rather than showing both participants. Root may hold if macro lacks enough visual context; do not count before independent review. Distinct conflict is her intended welcome versus mechanically responsive defense, rather than merely another spiky appearance joke.
