# Land regular 04

Six replacement proposals for existing image-less slots, not additional episodes. Four candidates and two explicit holds. No runtime changes from this document. These are regular posts, not visual Stories. Keep original episode indices and character homes if approved.

Eight photographs were independently displayed. Six are documented below as the closest scene matches; the other two were rejected during selection. The code/source/draft hash scan found no prior use of these originals. Recheck immediately before admission because other authors are active.

## 01. Cold all the way through

Nib Willow, `nib-willow`, red panda. Replace episode 8, **Rain underneath**. Instagram, weather. Candidate.

I licked the snow off a leaf. Cold. Licked it again to see whether it was cold all the way through. By the time I remembered the leaf, I had a wet beard and no new information.

Visible: red panda facing forward in snow, green bamboo leaves against its chest. Its feet are hidden by foreground snow; the photographer explicitly notes this. Fiction: licking, the experiment, and the resulting wet beard. The photo supplies the snow-and-leaf starting scene, not proof that its beard is wet. Preserve Nib's sleeping-branch history; this independent weather slot has no later rain callback. Mechanism: a creature's concrete experiment proceeds after the answer is already available. No claim that the animal was chilled or unhappy in snow.

## 02. Both ways smelled familiar

Sumi Smallhours, `sumi-smallhours`, pygmy slow loris. Replace episode 2, **Hand already occupied**. Facebook, movement. **Hold.**

I stopped in the fork to listen. By the time the noise went away, I had forgotten which side I was going toward. Put my nose to each branch. Both smelled of me. I had been very thorough without meaning to be helpful.

Visible: close loris face between two substantial branches. Fiction: interrupted journey, memory lapse, and scent inspection. Hold because the branch portrait cannot rescue another indecisive-movement anecdote, and the last sentence sounds composed for a punchline rather than lived. This is not an approved substitute. The original remains provisional. Do not invent a noise source or place a predator in the frame.

## 03. A plant with posture

Finch Fidget, `finch-fidget`, quokka. Replace episode 5, **The tough end**. Instagram, feeding. Candidate.

I pulled the shoot toward my mouth and it pulled back. Let go. It stood perfectly straight. I have asked plenty of plants to behave like food, but this one seems to want me to sit up first.

Visible: seated quokka holding a slender leafy shoot in both forepaws. The stem reaches down to ground vegetation rather than appearing as a loose mouthful. Fiction: bending, springing upright, and Finch's interpretation. Do not claim the roots are visible. Her mildly offended feeding voice survives without another generic complaint that her face looks happy. Mechanism: a rooted meal's springiness becomes an unsolicited correction of posture.

Continuity issue to resolve at admission: current episode 10, **The good end waited**, explicitly revisits episode 5's abandoned stem. Replacing 5 alone would orphan that callback. This proposal therefore requires either keeping 5 and using an independent slot such as episode 6, **Fresh dirt**, or reviewing 10 as part of a joint replacement. Preferred safe target is episode 6. The title and exact prose above are for that independent slot if approved.

## 04. A low opinion of winter

Bramble Shortlegs, `bramble-shortlegs`, common wombat. Potential replacement episode 2, **Very particular grass**. Facebook, weather. **Hold.**

I went out to find the grass. The snow reached my stomach before I had gone anywhere worth mentioning. I kept walking with the damp part of me held as high as it would go. It turns out I have been standing at full height for years.

Visible: common wombat among snow and exposed grass, snow flecks on back, short legs partly in snow. Fiction: destination, sensation, and attempted elevation. Hold because the exact photo is only 800 by 533, contains enclosure hardware, and is weaker visually than this library should accept. Also inspect whether the familiar short-legs premise is worth retaining before sourcing another image. Do not substitute the higher-resolution plain standing portrait reviewed as file 8; it removes the snowy belly scene that makes the text work.

## 05. The sound I moved

Mira Mask, `mira-mask`, raccoon. Replace episode 3, **An empty shell's possibilities**. Facebook, wonder. Candidate.

I moved a stone and the stream changed its voice. Put it back. Not quite the same voice. I have been nudging it with one finger for so long that I can no longer remember which sound I was trying to recover.

Visible: raccoon at a shallow rocky stream among moss, roots, and ferns. Stones visibly interrupt the flow. Fiction: the moved stone, altered sound, and prolonged adjustment. No hand-in-water action is documented by this frame. Main must judge the photograph as the scene of a remembered experiment, not certify the finger movement as visible. This does not change the distinct flat-stone flood sequence in episodes 5 and 9. Mechanism: tactile investigation turns into absorbed sound-making, with the original purpose quietly lost. No philosopher attribution or claim of scientific musical cognition.

## 06. The unfinished foot

Fern Longnose, `fern-longnose`, white-nosed coati. Replace episode 3, **An awkward descent**. Instagram, rest. Candidate.

I fell asleep halfway through climbing into the fork. Woke with my chin on the branch and a hind foot still looking for somewhere to go. I put the foot down. It found air. So I have gone back to sleep while it thinks of something else.

Visible: coati sprawled in a tree fork, head resting against the upper branch and a rear foot hanging below. Fiction: the interrupted climb, waking, testing the air, and returning to sleep. Do not insist that the eyes are closed; they are open in the exact image. This proposal illustrates the waking portion. Preserve her curiosity, group connections, and the recurring fallen-log sequence. Mechanism: sleep interrupts movement so thoroughly that one limb's unfinished business can wait. No unseen admirer, ignored suffering, or a new house.

## Biological and attribution checks

Fresh primary-source lookup returned [Smithsonian's red panda snow account](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/news/meet-red-pandas-asa-and-chris-anne), supporting comfortable cold-weather activity, and its [species account](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/red-panda). Do not write a cold-distress narrative from the snowy photograph. Its source locality is Planckendaal Zoo, Belgium; the fictional home remains Singalila.

[Duke Lemur Center](https://lemur.duke.edu/pygmy-slow-loris/) describes arboreal pygmy slow lorises and tree-gum feeding. It is also the direct photographic source of the loris portrait. Current taxonomy may use *Xanthonycticebus*; do not invent a new species for Sumi or imply a North Carolina photo was captured at Cat Tien.

[Rottnest Island Authority](https://www.rottnestisland.com/see-do/wildlife-nature/Quokkas) is the primary local source for the quokka setting. The image itself identifies Rottnest, consistent with Finch's canonical home. No feeding by humans is introduced.

The raccoon photo is an NPS image, credited to Kevin Bacher at Mount Rainier. It illustrates Mira's fictional stream scene without relocating her Great Smoky Mountains home. Text makes no unsupported anatomical claim or folk claim that raccoons wash all food. Coati source identifies Tulum, Mexico; Fern's fictional Corcovado home stays unchanged. All personified thoughts and temporal actions are fiction, not behavioral study results.
