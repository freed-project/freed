## Without a signal

I had expected the cone to feel lighter when it opened. After two summers of holding the seeds, I thought I would notice them leaving.

The wind moved the whole branch. Needles rubbed together. The cone tapped the twig beside it.

When everything settled, its scales were still open.

I cannot tell you which gust carried the last seed. This bothers me more than I expected. I had been there for all the waiting.

The younger cones are still shut. I keep hoping one of them will make a sound when it is time.
