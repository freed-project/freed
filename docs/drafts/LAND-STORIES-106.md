## Biology and rights boundaries

[NPS Yellowstone beaver account](https://www.nps.gov/yell/learn/nature/beaver.htm) supports the species and gnawing trees on the bank. It does not certify the invented creaks, remaining structural strength or Berta's response. No technical tree-felling advice is embedded in the passage. The two bird scenes use ordinary locomotion, looking and preening, not a novel biological claim. Inca tern ornaments do not by themselves establish sex.

Each chosen primary photo object explicitly gives CC BY and links CC BY 4.0. Delivery variants are its published `large_url`, downloaded without transformation. Attribution and license must travel with any later admission. A photograph's real location is provenance, not a fictional travel record.
