## Continuity before prose

Selma remains the adult California sea lion mother from San Miguel Island. Her earlier pup scratch is a marked memory, her own surfing accident is spent, and stopping movement to accommodate the pup is spent. Source01 therefore uses the pictured foreground spectator, while source03 develops her established attachment to the pup's voice in a crowded breeding group. Della remains the common bottlenose dolphin whose small competitions conceal her wish to stay with others. Her paired breath-matching and leap-length contest are spent. Source05 changes the competition through close group contact. Source06 is a new dangerous misunderstanding, without a suffering joke or invented rescue.

Florida-source dolphin photographs remain species-level illustrations under Della's Kona home. They are not travel evidence or license to import regional learned hunting techniques. NOAA explicitly calls these selected animals common bottlenose dolphins; nearby article images identified as Tamanend's dolphins were not substituted. No new photograph passed for Tobin or Fenn.
