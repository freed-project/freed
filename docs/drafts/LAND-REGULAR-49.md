## 01. Eustace Peel: Something happened to the wood

Facebook regular. Adult-sized male North American porcupine, Erethizon dorsatum, Noatak National Preserve. Sex and precise age are fictional, not source-recorded.

The wall tasted so good that I went outside to see whether something had happened to the trees.

Bit a branch. Chewed well into it. Ordinary wood.

Back inside, I put my teeth under the pale edge and pulled. A thin sheet came away. Another layer underneath, with that same extraordinary taste.

I sat down in the shavings and started on the next layer.
