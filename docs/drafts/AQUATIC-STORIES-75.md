## Image, fiction and canon

The frame shows two compatible Nembrotha bodies facing opposite directions in sustained contact. The departure, small gap, mutual turn, desire to restore contact and silence are authored fiction. The source records a dive location and depth, not an observed behavioral sequence or mating event. Do not label genital coupling as visible.

Existing Anilao Nembrotha kubaryana, hermaphroditic, ascidian-feeding chemical defense. Primary observation is Mainit Corner, Mabini, Batangas, matching the established Anilao setting. No new sex assignment, egg history, partner identity or diet change.

Propose after We both brought everything as a later encounter. The unnamed companion need not be the previously pictured partner. No approved wording or avatar changes.

Existing mating scene concerns logistical orientation; this scene concerns choosing to restore contact after an ordinary passing encounter. Reversal is mutual rather than a final joke about anatomy. Lead should hold if this is too close to existing intimacy or if the picture suggests a specific reproductive event that makes the passing interpretation implausible.

Primary biological reference: [Sea Slug Forum Nembrotha kubaryana](https://www.seaslugforum.net/showall/nembkuba). Current source identification and precise location come from the primary observation, not a visual genus guess. All current Nudi episodes and species canon were read before drafting.
