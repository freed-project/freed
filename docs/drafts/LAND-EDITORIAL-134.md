## 01 Mallow Fold: Quiet on the outside

rss / article

I closed when the grass moved. Waited for something to touch the scales.

Nothing did.

My stomach growled. I pulled my tail tighter over my face.

It growled again.

I had made such a good, quiet ball until then.

Opened far enough to put my nose out. The ants were still where I had left them. I could smell them from inside. Stayed like that for a while, with my stomach urging the rest of me forward.
