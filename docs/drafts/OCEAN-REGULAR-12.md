# Ocean regular 12: bounded image review

Three Rhea drafts are recommended for independent editorial review. No runtime admission is claimed. The initial search aimed at four scenes each for Nellie, Rhea, Rue and Mina; the final deliverable does not pretend that target was met. Fourteen exact files were opened individually. Ten additional sources returned HTTP 429 pages and were not viewed. The lead authorized closing this as a bounded batch without further retries.

## Continuity and biological evidence

Current ocean arcs, the admitted Nellie fish passage, Rue's breathing and neighbor stories, and Mina's working canon were read. Existing identities and fictional homes remain unchanged. Rhea source photographs are from Oman, Egypt and the Philippines; they illustrate the same identified species, not documented travel by the fictional Little Cayman turtle. No individual animal continuity or Caribbean location is claimed in image metadata.

[NOAA Fisheries](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/hawksbill-turtle?page=1) describes hawksbill reef habitat, sponge feeding, and resting shelter beneath ledges and in caves. [Sazima and colleagues, 2010](https://www.scielo.br/j/ni/a/PvqvkD8tSvfKhMvCpSWrZky/?lang=en) document reef fish feeding on hawksbill epibionts. That supports a cleaning scene generally; it does not identify the pictured fish or prove each pictured mouthful. [USFWS Florida manatee account](https://www.fws.gov/apps/carp/species/florida-manatee-trichechus-manatus-latirostris) supports the subspecies and air-breathing constraints.

The three drafts use physical scenes to support imagined short events. A source need not record the whole fictional sequence. Source facts and narrative invention remain separate below.

> A school came past while I was swimming. I kept going at my speed. Now I am inside the school, and everyone is making a great many adjustments.

Rhea keeps her own pace while the smaller bodies accommodate her. Unlike Nellie holding a flipper for small fish, she has made no deliberate concession.
