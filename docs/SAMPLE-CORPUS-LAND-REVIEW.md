# Land character draft and review

## Admission status

September 4, 2026: 150 individually authored episodes from 15 new characters are saved in `packages/shared/src/sample-character-arcs-land.ts`. Fifty are longer RSS stories. The owner subsequently approved an intentional photo and text-only mixture. Four visually reviewed scene images are provided in `packages/shared/src/sample-corpus-land-media.json` and bound to their specific episodes; the remaining 146 are explicitly text-only with null bindings. The helper attaches subject metadata to static authored text; it does not generate or combine prose.

Mechanical checks: 150 distinct titles, 150 distinct bodies, all titles within nine words and 58 characters, all X bodies within 280 characters, no title repeated verbatim inside its body. These checks do not certify humor or image fit.

The author read the complete editorial contract including the rejected bee passage. No episode treats prey distress as its comic mechanism. There is one courtship episode, no philosophical quotations, and no location names in bodies. Reviewed against the no-ai-slop checklist. Self-review removed explanatory last lines from Juniper and Sumi, replaced Orla's generic comfort conclusion with a remembered twig, and replaced a second tail-entanglement incident in Fern's arc.

## Final author audit

All 150 land entries received a complete additional text read for a concrete comic turn, emotional consequence, anatomical plausibility, and continuity. Comparative reading of the original approved examples and air/ocean drafts exposed repeated mechanisms that were not literal sentence duplicates. Eleven entries were rewritten in this pass:

- Nib 4: replaced another leaf false alarm with a witnessed scent-marking embarrassment.
- Juniper 9: replaced the wrong-burrow visit with forgetting that the neighbor also has exceptional hearing.
- Sumi 3: replaced the leaf-dumps-rain gag used elsewhere with wet fur exposing an alarmingly thin but competent wrist.
- Sumi 7: replaced unsupported flower-to-flower transfer with accidental pollen spread across her own face and ear.
- Sumi 8: replaced a wandering-ant interruption with alternating hands that keep touching the sticky meal.
- Sumi 9: replaced successful passage followed by a twig collision with choosing to stay after passage is possible.
- Orla 4: removed the gravel-mouth mechanism already owned by the approved Frogbert example.
- Tully 9: made vigilance cost him the specific leaves he harvested, rather than ending on a generic breakfast delay.
- Wren 5: replaced an abstract statement of ground anxiety with taking the safe route and missing her partner at both ends.
- Mira 5: replaced a flood observation with abandoning a dry ledge to investigate, then making her replacement seat wet herself.
- Nessa 2: removed following her own tracks, which duplicated Percy's feeding trail. The gull now diverts her from a cache whose position she actually knows.

This is an author audit, not a claim that every reader will find every passage funny. Repeated natural behaviors remain, including feeding, shelter and awkward social approaches, but the revisions target identical reversals rather than banning shared biology. No prey suffering is the punchline. No invented quotations or philosophical attribution appear. Original approved passages remain untouched. Fifty RSS entries span events rather than becoming fifty versions of a short gag. Kit and Nessa have seasonal stories; their relative display dates must not be read as a literal claim that seasons changed within a few days.

Mechanical recheck: 150 episodes, 150 unique titles, 150 unique bodies, 50 RSS stories, zero X bodies over 280 characters, zero titles over nine words or 58 characters, and `git diff --check` passed. Runtime admission and production validation belong to the integration owner.
