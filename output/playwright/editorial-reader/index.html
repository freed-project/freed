He kept offering to teach me to swim. I let him explain it while I walked beside him. Eventually he spread his fins and demonstrated. Very graceful. He came back looking pleased. I could have swum then. I had been able to swim throughout. But nobody had ever gone to so much trouble to spend another minute with me.
